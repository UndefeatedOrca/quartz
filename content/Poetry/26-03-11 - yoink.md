Attempting minor thievery
Red-handed I was caught
By friend who thought I shouldn't take
The things I haven't got

I guess that's right, but also, well
It's funny larceny
A running bit begun somehow
In distant history

But foiled again in my pursuit
I bide my time and wait
Until the window of a chance
Unveils for different fate
