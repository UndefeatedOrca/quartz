Jingle goes a line of hair
The bells are all unstayed
Just ringing merrily as we're
Continuing to play

Then suddenly, extended F
A buzzing emanates
From bells embedded in the horn
Which seem to resonate

The section breaks off from the band
To laugh and gasp and choke
Then back to business and sound
And nary word is spoke
