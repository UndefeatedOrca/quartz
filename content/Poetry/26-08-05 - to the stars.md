Gleaming blocks of chiseled stone
An ivory thoroughfare 
That towers up into the deep
That goes through depths of air

It's paving only goes so far 
Part way to galaxies 
On either side is sheer abyss
To silent stormy seas

What pavers started long ago
Foundation firmly set
The generations tred upon 
Extended and upset

The narrow way is ours to build 
For although we have strode
The time has come to make the path 
And to extend the road
