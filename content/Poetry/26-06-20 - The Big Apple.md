The cars are honking violently 
Pedestrians ignore
And bicycles go running down
Fast through the streets they pour

Stoplights are optional with grit 
To merge requires shove 
And vendors crowd the busy streets
While towers are above

It's anarchy, it's joy, it's pain
It isn't great for cars 
And if you want to catch the game
There's no shortage of bars

The crazy things that you will find
If you, that can, uncork
Are wonderful and dangerous 
And most of all, New York
