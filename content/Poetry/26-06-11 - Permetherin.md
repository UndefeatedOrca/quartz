Insecticide is on my clothes
My socks, and pants, and shirts
So I do not get alpha-gal
Or any other hurts

Whether or not conspiracy's
Behind infectious ticks
With treated clothing I will live
And give the bugs their licks

(My only hope is that now applied
I did the whole thing right
And didn't fill my lungs with it
To enter greater plight)
