While walking peacefully along
I must have stumbled some
And sloshed a bit of tea on me
You know me, I'm oft dumb

So somehow I squeezed on the cup
The sudden movement spurred
A larger helping of my drink
Onto my hand disturbed

And with that searing pain I dropped
The whole accursed thing
To spill onto the sidewalk stone
Amidst the awful sting

This message I will bring you now
To others future warn
That cups from Dunkin aren't the best
Watch out to not get burned
