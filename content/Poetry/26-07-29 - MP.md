Without a glimpse of light at all
The seeker glides along
With momentary calls to see
If he is going wrong

His prey, compelled to speak to him
Replies in simple burst
A Polo coming from their mouths
When he says Marco first

And while they hide under the waves
They can't remain like that
So soon enough the seeker finds
A new one's at the bat
