For breakfast I will let you choose
The content of your meal
I hope that you are wise in that
So you won't sit and reel

For some of these are not alike
They're savory, they're bad
There's bacon in a few of them
But some will make you sad

I somehow think that you won't like
The ones a'filled with cheese
They might even create in you
Distress in you to quease

But on the other hand the fruit
And jelly is quite good
Nutella hides inside a few
I'd grab those if I could

Just stay away from salsa ones
And steer yourself toward fruits
The evilskivers aren't the best
But ebelskevers? Beauts
