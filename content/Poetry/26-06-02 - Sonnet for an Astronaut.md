Her hair is darker than the deepest night
The solar power dims to see her smile
Her eyes reflect the same stars' brilliant light
As will illume my own despites the miles
The nothing separates me from my love
No obstacle stands in the way of reaching her
Mere million vacant spaces up above
Each step more difficult to take I'm sure
Connection that goes past the moon and back
Your presence could fill galaxies and more
So while for precious moments you I lack
And while endured the distance heart is sore
Although up to the stars you did depart
You'll always have a place here in my heart
