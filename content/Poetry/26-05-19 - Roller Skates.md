Just strap the wheels on to your feet
And you'll be moving quick
Assuming you can stay upright
And ground and feet will stick

Then get into the oval rink 
And circle round the rim 
And watch out for the other ones
Who dart on through the dim

And doing should come easily 
But will you stop with grace 
Or will you stop the other way
And wind up on your face?
