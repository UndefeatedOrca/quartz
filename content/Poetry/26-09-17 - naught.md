Though [courage rose](25-2-14-Valentine)
And flowers brought
It seems that it
Was all for naught

The long-sought day
Ends in a puff
An outcome that
I'd say is rough

And though I've said
The [ball game's](26-07-25-newinning) long
I wonder if
That turned out wrong

Persistence now
Will tantalize
But I was wrong
When I surmised

To dare to hope
And not to rhyme
Detritus from
Another time

Comparison
Is thief of joy
But I can't help
But it employ

For there is none
Who can come close
To she, but I
Won't be verbose

The only thing
That's left to say
Is "[we](24-9-8-Welp)[lp](24-9-9-Welppt.2)" this wasn't
Quite my day

---
Perhaps I lied concluding here
For lining storm clouds is some cheer
In killing all uncertainty
And forcing hope to leave me be
But though I wish, I lack the might
To drown in agony's delight
For darkness cometh before dawn
And peace is forecasted in yawns
But nothing's ever stayed the same
'cept death and taxes and God's name
A final setback this may be
But I know there are plans for me
