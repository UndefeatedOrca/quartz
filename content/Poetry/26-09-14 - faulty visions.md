Dreams and visions have their place
To fill mind's elsewise empty space
But trusting them to tell the truth
Is a mistake of foolish youth
Coincidence abounds throughout
Forgotten is a cause for doubt
And bias fills them through and through
So I won't trust the ones of you
That tell a tale to cause me grief
That steal my hope away, a thief
Experience of brighter day
Shall have the only final say
And disappointment may still come
But dreaming it ahead is dumb
