I've made it back, and in one piece
Back to this school's ground
But there are things I didn't pack
Which really can't be found

Umbrella, raincoat missing are
My toothbrush I forgot
Though travel tube will hold me down
Until in comes new lot

I brought my teapot, that was fine
But didn't bring the tea
And tissues were invisible
Forbid that I should sneeze

I didn't bring the loose leaf sheets
I had in yesteryear
Or quite as much memo pads
Had packing been more clear

And finally, my second screen
Though back and in one piece
Does not possess the cables which
Would images release

So overall I'm doing well
Unless it starts to rain
Or tea, or papercraft demand
If that, then there'll be pain
