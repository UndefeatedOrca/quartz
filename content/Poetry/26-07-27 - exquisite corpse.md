Devised with artistry and craft
The painting's beauty glows
Until the second man is up
Disrupting old workflows

He doesn't look to see what's made
Just grabs the palette cup
And gets to work on torso art
Without a mere glance up

The third guy also comes right in
Devises a new scheme
To fill the bottom third right in
And now, nightmare, not dream
