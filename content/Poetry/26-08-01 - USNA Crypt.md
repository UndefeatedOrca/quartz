The crypt is dim with somber light
Remembering a man who'd fight
The British in their ships at sea
While we were only colonies
And when they thought the battle done
He told them that he'd just begun
The father of the Naval force
They lost his body in the course
Of history, but is was found
And now it's in this very round
Where you can find the hallowed bones
Of legend Captain John Paul Jones
