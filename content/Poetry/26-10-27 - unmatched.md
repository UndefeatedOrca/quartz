The trumpet sounds, the herald cries
And all the conversation dies
Now up the stairs all gazes fix
Anticipation, joy all mix
As doors swing wide, a glint of crown 
Is visible, she's coming down
Yes coming down the winding stair 
Is princess with a face so fair
That kings and princes everywhere
Have warred for just a lock of hair
Her gown composed of bands of gold
Which sparkle, glimmer, bend, and fold
Wrapping lissome lovely frame
Deserving all its great acclaim
But somewhere lonely down below
A lesser noble thinks in woe
How after seeing her bright light
All other things will seem as night
To her unreachable delight
