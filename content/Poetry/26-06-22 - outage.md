The power has gone out on us
The candles have burned down
Now sleep will come, and with it hope
Tomorrow holds no frown

Without the ceiling lights to see
Without fan-cooled air
The night may end up rather long
We'll see just how things fair

But as for me, I go to bed
In standard state I guess
My bedroom has no airflow now
Nor then I must confess
