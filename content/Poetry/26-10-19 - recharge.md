Batteries charge when they're plugged in
And die when they are not
The people are like that with their sleep
Burn out with too much thought
But how do you charge a weary soul
How do they find repose
Some find their rest in fellowship
While others in silent flows
In reading a book, uplifting or Good
In spending some time alone
But uniting all of these ways to recharge
Is staying away from a phone
