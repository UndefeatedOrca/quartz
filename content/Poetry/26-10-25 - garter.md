A garnish to a stylish friend
It winds along his arm
A red and yellow twisting thing
But no cause for alarm

It seems to move a little bit
Manipulated wend
But what is it that's moving it?
What causes it to bend

Unless, oh my, oh deary me
That is no ornament
It is in fact a living snake
Around his arm that's bent
