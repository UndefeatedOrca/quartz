My ice cream bill is stacking up
I'm sorta worried here
My friends keep going out for it
And I say yes I fear

Locations have been varied too
Beyond the Cone is one
But going down to CraveAbles
Was also kinda fun

I missed out on a Rookies run
But Mr Goodies made
And all of this for frozen joy
Has full, with cash, been paid

Though none are large just on their own
These things seem to add up
But on the other hand, I guess
I could get one more cup
