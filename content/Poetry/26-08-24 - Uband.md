Surrounded by a sea of sound
By waves of melody
Ensconced in avenues, delight
And filled with harmony

A rising scale crescendos up
Claps down to piano
Then builds to higher heights again
Just moving with the flow

Glissando, accent, tempo change
Scherzando in there too
Awash again in music's light
There I find joy that's true
