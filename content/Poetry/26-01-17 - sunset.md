Upon the moons of Jupiter
The paintbrush is not stayed
And gentle dappled red and green
Upon horizons play

The Martian sky is filled with light
The beams go streaming down
The sunrise is a sight to see
Up on the mountain crown

And Venus has oppressive clouds
That shade the surface but
In middle of the acid rains
You'll still see light abut

But Earth, the jewel of them all
Receives my favorite art
The dazzle reds and purple hues
My home is set apart

I'm biased in experience
But I will surely say
That sunsets here are lovelier
Than aliens can say
