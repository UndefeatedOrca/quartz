Although I've done some epic things
I think I've got to say
Creative Cloud's removal was
The best thing of today

Who cares compared to this:
I no longer have Photoshop
And it will not be missed

(My license had expired back
In 2022)
And Acrobat is gone as well
In happiness: WOO HOO!
