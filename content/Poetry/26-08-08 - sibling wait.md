The conference done 
Down is the sun
I sleep tonight
Before my flight
But ere I doze
Or early rose
I must await
My sister's gait
To get the things
For her to bring
So I can fly
Into the sky
Without a suit 
And only boot
Without checked bag
Only jet lag 

When she gets back
From the Shake Shack
