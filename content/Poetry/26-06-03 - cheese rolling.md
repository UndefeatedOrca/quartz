The Double Gloucester ribboned up
[Descends](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooper%27s_Hill_Cheese-Rolling_and_Wake) each fateful year
Down Cooper's hill in Gloucestershire
But do not, ye, have fear

For noble souls descend with it
To catch it ere it breaks
Upon the crowd of spectators
And shatters into flakes

They're running down the great incline
Some faster and some slow
Some are awake and conscious but
[One's knocked out down below](https://www.theguardian.com/food/2023/may/29/woman-wins-coopers-hill-cheese-rolling-race-despite-being-knocked-out)

[Chris Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Anderson_(cheese_roller)) is running too
And he's leading the pack
Though cheddar all he wants to eat
He won't gloucester lack

What you won't see are ambuli
It's unofficial now
But what counts isn't government
It's people going, WOW
