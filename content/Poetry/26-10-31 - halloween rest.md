Hilloween is over
Shoeoween is done
Tower's Terror ended
No more is the fun

Pumpkins will be bundled
Tossed and thrown away
Unless they aren't minded
Rotting in hallways

Masks and paints are off now
Wigs are simply gone
Now we wait for morning
And the new time's dawn

Hallows Eve is ended
Soon the bells will ring
For the saints departed
Who in hea'en now sing
