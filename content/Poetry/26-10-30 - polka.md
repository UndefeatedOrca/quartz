Do you hear the beat and time
As music plays and feet will fly
The time is driving twos and fours
Sometimes threes but not the norm
With just a hint of offbeat kick
To take the polka dance to full
And try to keep the form alive
Without collapsing on a foot
Or stumbling into other men
Or crashing into women too
It's just a little awkward
