Accumulated in my room
Are years and years of notes
From conferences and lecture, talks
I carefully handwrote

But now it's time to digitize
Those letters one by one
Until after so many words
The job is finally done

Will it get done this summer though?
Last summer I had planned
To do this, but it didn't work
I hope you understand

That this year's got a different strat
Called simply doing it
Instead of writing up the list
Then saying: "in a bit"
