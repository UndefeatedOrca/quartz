Friends are dear and lovely too
They're filled with laughter's jest
Provoking joy on evening trips
They simply are the best

Companionship which they provide
And comfort when in pain
Though they take time to cultivate
The end result is gain

I cannot right express my love
For who I call friend
But what I'll say is that I hope
That never we will end
