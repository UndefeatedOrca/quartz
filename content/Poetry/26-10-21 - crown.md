Lurking in the dusty path
A crown is on the ground
Directions not, nor any map
It's waiting to be found

Received by those who wind through mist
Defeating those who claim
The throne and power that it holds
The holder ends their aim

But who shall tread the unblazed way
To stir the pot of fog
And end the machinations of
Those who wish dark and smog
