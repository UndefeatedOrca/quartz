I resent the system that
Demands I stay up late
To register for what I need
To walk and graduate

I resent the system that
Has held me to midnight
Before proceeding to implode
With no thought to my plight

I resent the system that
Is loading here right now
But isn't getting anywhere
No matter curse nor vow

I resent the system yet
I'm still awake today
Which used to be tomorrow
In the sunlit hour of day
