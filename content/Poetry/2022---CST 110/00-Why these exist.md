So, CST 110 was a mind numbing class and Professor Walter was kinda crazy in a way that made me not take the class very seriously, so I decided the best way to keep myself engaged in class was to take a stream of consciousness approach to notetaking with the only constraint being that everything had to be written in Haiku. The result was better than anything I expected, and the process makes for a funny story, if only because I somehow managed to keep up the process through the entire class without giving up (though if you look carefully, you might notice that the length varies wildly, mainly owing to how awake I was on any given day).

These poems were transcribed by hand from their original notebook. Minor edits for grammatic and spelling accuracy have been made, but the goal in transcription was to keep the original text to the greatest degree possible.
