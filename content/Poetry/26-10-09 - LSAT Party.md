Finally finished, finally done
Finally able to have some fun
But where shall we journey, where shall we go
Where shall we venture, I do not know
How about cookout, how about food
How about dancing while we're in the mood
What about Snowflex, what if they're closed
What would you say to the horse's abode
Look up to the stars, yea look up there and gaze
Look out for the deer as our sanity frays
But beware the med school and beware the cop
Beware attempting to park at the top
Consider a new place, consider North Suites
Consider that they haven't got any heat
But finally slow down and finally rest
We're finally finally done with the test
