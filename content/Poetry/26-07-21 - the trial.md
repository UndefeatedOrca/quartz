I hear a rumbling in the deep
Where scourge and titans gently sleep
A beast awakens from its rest
To put the mighty to the test
It stretches out a gnarled claw
Which only ancient sages saw
Uprooted from the eons, torn
It surges up as if reborn
And now it rises from the gorge
To be the stuff of hero's forge
