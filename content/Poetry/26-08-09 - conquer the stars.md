What love is worth the moniker
That doesn't seek to rise
What love is worth the very name
That doesn't seek a prize

What love is worthy of a man
But one that he must win
What love is worthy to be true 
But one without chagrin

What love is more superior
Devotion to taller
Than love that's always looking up
To virtue, beauty, her
