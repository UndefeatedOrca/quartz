I have a bench to sit on now
Composed of treated wood
I made it for my crafting class
The time was rather good

I'm not quite sure I made it right
But that will be alright
I wasn't aiming for the moon
When screws I put in tight

And after all, so far it's still
Content and in one piece
It could be worse, it could be gone
And then where'd be my peace
