They called me in for conference work
To babysitting do
But only in the final hour
Did I learn what was true

They called me to move move boxes out
And help clean up the mess
Which had been laid out through it all
Not my fault I confess

Not I've been called to campaign work
And also now its true
That moving boxes is my job
And what I've had to do

(But in the morn when votes shall come
I hear that there is more
But what exactly it shall be
Who knows what is in store)

---
Today I learned, among other things, just how useful a pallet cart is compared to hand carts and dolleys and what 25,000 t-shirts and 10,000 baseball hats looks like.
