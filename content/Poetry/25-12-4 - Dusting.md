While walking through the chilly night
I felt a twinge of wet
My coat was very slightly damp
The reason hidden yet

I wandered past a lamppost tall
And saw a flickering
Precipitation in the air
Was slowly down drifting

Intensifying as I walked
The sleet turned into snow
To bring a joy to all outside
In gentle moonlit glow
