The day is coming very soon 
When I can cut my hair
And I am free of burden-locks
At which I sometimes tear

(Though tearing is a consequence
Of other things in life
Like scheduling and planning trips
Which has been recent strife)

We'll see exactly how it goes
But if it goes too bad
I'll maybe get another buzz
Though it would make me sad
