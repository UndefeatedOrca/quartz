Too optimistic for one night
I start composing late
To finish on the morrow might
Depending on my fate

But whether that will be the case
Depends on how things go
Tomorrow and in latter days
And if the story flows

For Stewart may be deep in love
Rolinda may be not
If that's the case then Stewart's heart
May just be left to rot

But I suspect if all else fails
All else is less than all
So Stewart will arise again
Despite a painful fall
