The work is done until week's end
The essay is turned in
Now I can go and read a book
Or take bike on a spin

I've already had fun this week
With hot dogs after church
Tomorrow's looking dreary though
And school's in a lurch

The rain will come with epic wrath
The temperatures will drop
It isn't great, at least I know
The outdoor plans to stop

But just like that the break will end
And I'll be back at school
Where hopefully the spring has sprung
And things won't be so cool
