I think if Aristotle had
A Ford F-150
He'd drive it up the Grecian hills
And ride along the sea

Admiring craftsmanship and art
While burning gasoline
Not pondering the way
He'd made it to the scene

If Plato drove an APC
Across the Aegean
He wouldn't think about mileage
Would simply take the win

Deploying personnel
With new technology
Perhaps to conquer Carthage
(Too early for Rome's See)

And Socrates and Boeing
747 Max
Wouldn't consider safety
Or high property tax

But take it to the regions
No man had been before
Yes if he had a jumbo jet
I think he'd make it soar
