The slot machine is flashing by
What pill will you consume
Of ignorance perhaps, or truth
Or maybe one of doom

Just pull a lever and you'll have
The liberty from choice
To see what is reality 
Just sacrifice your voice

The house has rigged the game itself
There's nothing you can say
The only winning choice you have
Is choosing not to play 

The gambler doesn't build a house
Or plant a single tree
The only way for him to win
Is choosing to be free
