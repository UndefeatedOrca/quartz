In times of darkness and of pain
Where hope is lost to fear
There is a place that you can turn
There is a man to hear

He also once was overwhelmed
By sorrows He would weep
Despite that He would go to pain
His promises to keep

But what a triumph He has had
Join with the triumph cry
The work is finished on a cross
For it a man would die

The story doesn't end at that
There's more to tell to you
For He has risen from the grave
And someday you will too

This present darkness deepens still
But you are not alone
When words have failed, the Spirit wails
Too deep for words, He groans

Turn to our God in faith and ask
My soul what do you fear
Our God is mighty and he reigns
Love, praise Him through the tears
