``The table rotates silky smooth
The drawers full of parts
To reassemble what was there
And make a piece of art

Assorted into many parts
The edges scrutinized
As tests are made to every piece
Mistakes are gently prized

The progress made was rather good
Substantial I might say
Tomorrow maybe it will be
Completed all the way
