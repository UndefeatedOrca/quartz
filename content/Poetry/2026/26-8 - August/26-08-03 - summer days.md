The summer days are closing soon
To be replaced by school
With days in classrooms and with books
With weather that is cool

The summer days are closing soon
The pool is almost done
The courses will begin again
Perhaps it will be fun

While summer days are closing soon
They haven't ended yet
So cherish even humid days
And cherish late sunsets
