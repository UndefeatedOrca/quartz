While packing has gone rather well
And I've new kicks and pants
That doesn't mean I'm running out
Of cause for my rants

Cause somehow late last night I had
A fairy visit me
And curse me with an evil thing
I didn't want to see

A Microsoft sent curse I had
An update I suspect
Which damaged some poor registry
With evil to infect

So when I booted up again
The error message showed
Which warned of locking out a drive
If missteps I soon strode

But then things seemed to fix themselves
Until I opened up
My email, oh to think of it
It had a slight hiccup

That is to say, it didn't work
And try as though I might
My efforts to repair the thing
Were not to work, not quite

Until I stumbled on a thing
That might have done the trick
Amid the frequent restarts
Well, I guess it did the trick

Repairing from control panel
Was the last thing I did
Before the thing was fixed at last
Of bugs I was then rid
