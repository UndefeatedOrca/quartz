Mount Vernon seen from far above
The clouds are just below
And National is coming soon
As fast as airplanes go

The monuments are up ahead
Masonic Temple back
And now the wheels are going down
Onto the asphalt track

The flight has landed finally
After a few delays
And now I'm going back to school
In just a few short days
