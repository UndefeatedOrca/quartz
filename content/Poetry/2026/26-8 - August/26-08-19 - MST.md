The Rockies are a lovely place
I'm glad I visited
But in one aspect I'm afraid
They greatly me misled

The time out there is slightly off
Off what's here in the East
Combined with poor clock checking skills
Have now unleashed a beast

My sleep schedule is out of whack
Which really isn't great
A curfew might improve things but
Right now I'm up too late
