While many of the passerbys
Are mysteries to me
A few of them are lovely friends
That I am glad to see

They tell me stories of their lives
And I can share in turn
We talk of things to do or not
(We mutually warn)

United once again are we
On campus for the year
Right now is when there is no work
And so there's space for cheer
