The peanut butter's on the knife
Like many times before
Creating sandwiches for food
And then creating more

Another stands with jelly there
To finish off the meal
And slowly I am starting to
Exhausted my knees feel

But I enjoy the simple work
And keep a cheerful mood
For I have been provided and
Hope others too have food
