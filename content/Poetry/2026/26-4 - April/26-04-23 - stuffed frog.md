A frog is on a lampstand and
He's sitting very still
He's contemplating what he does
While watching window sill

Although he's stuffed he dreams of what
He'd do if he could leap
But when it gets down to the truth
His perch he still would keep

But somewhere in the depths of soul
He wonders if there's more
He's wonders if he'd make the jump
With courage to explore
