The fog is rolled in draping sheets
Just vague lights can be seen
With only amorphous shape
Compiled of lights mean

The blurs obscure what once was clear
Confuse the wanderers
And riders in the dead of night
Refrain from using spurs

The moon is somewhere up there but
The stars have disappeared
The night is something from a dream
Or is it a nightmare?
