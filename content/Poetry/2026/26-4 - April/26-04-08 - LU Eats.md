The mobile order app is great
I hold very dear
But sometimes it is very bad
At telling me things clear

Today I ordered up some bread
And meat, toppings, and cheese
Then I was told "almost ready"
For as long as you please

In fact I checked the app just now
Five hours after first
I sent my order in to it
But this is not the worst

For though the app claims its not done
My stomach disagrees
For I did really quite enjoy
My fresh roast beef and cheese
