Tomfoolery, it's time to build
Some mischief to release
Before the year is ended and
The goofiness will cease

Despite what I have said a lot
I still think that there's fun
To take from games that overall
I think that should be done

So while the deadlines come on fast
One final twist remains
Until retirement is full
We'll see what is ordained
