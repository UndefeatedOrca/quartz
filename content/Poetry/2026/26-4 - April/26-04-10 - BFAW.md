I met my boyfriend Thursday night
Just sitting near the rot
His friendly face and brilliant smile
Just ripped away my thought

I introduced myself to him
He asked me on a date
I asked him what his major was
And saw him hesitate

He said that he does lots of things
Is graduating soon
And that was all enough for me
I practically swooned

We dined in bliss I'd never seen
His talk was full of thought
He picked his food with oh such care
As if the first he'd got

I asked him where his dorm room was
Off-campus though, he claims
Which all the better I believe
For he's a car and aims

He showed up to my classes too
Though he still needs a map
Perhaps he's simply tired and
Just only needs a nap

I texted him today but still
He hasn't yet replied
I asked around to find him but
By no one he's been spied

I looked him up on Instagram
A doppelganger found
His lookalike's in high school
That turned me all around

Perhaps what really happened is
The BFAW that I made
Is really someone who is in
The young eleventh grade
