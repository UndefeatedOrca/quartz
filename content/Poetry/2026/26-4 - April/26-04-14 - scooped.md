Captured on my way to dorm 
An innocent I was
Careened away to certain doom 
Unrescued by the fuzz 

Away away out of the gates
To distant mystery 
A bag upon my confused head 
It will not let me see

But maybe things will work out though
Perhaps I'll be okay
It's possible my friends have planned
Surprise for my birthday
