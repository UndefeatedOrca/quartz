Release the hounds of Artemis
Make swift their midnight run
As they revolve around the Earth
More swiftly than the Sun

Release the hounds of Artemis
And give them tongues of flame
Deploy them to the distant reach
They shan't return the same

Release the hounds of Artemis
Don't let them sleeping lie
Release the hounds of Artemis
For they were born to fly
