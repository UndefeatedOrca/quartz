The darkness covered all the earth
Creation filled with dread
Creator of the world was there
Up on that cross hanged dead

The curtain tore, finality
It's finished He had said
But now His side is being speared
Although they know He's dead

And now they give His body up
Now that His life is shed
He will be put into a tomb
But will He remain dead?
