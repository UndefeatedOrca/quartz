Across the net the ball has flown
A thwap, I hit it back
Returned to us by other team
My partner hits it, thwack

A softer bounce will bring it back
A simple little tap
But now I spike it straight back down
A whistling loud thwap

The first bounce lies within the lines
But far out of the reach
Of those who would have returned it
But their defense it breached
