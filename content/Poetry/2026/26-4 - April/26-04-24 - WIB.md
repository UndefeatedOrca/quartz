While it isn't my usual
Food service isn't bad
When Women in (the) Business club
Waitering needs are had

I dress in black, don my bowtie
And go to gala spot
To bring the plate to ladies who
Know business a whole lot

And after that, the fellas go
We have a bit of fun
With dinner eaten and then cleared
Our job is finally done
