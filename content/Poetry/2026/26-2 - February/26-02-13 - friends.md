My friends I do appreciate
They keep me barely sane
Walk with me in the sunny times
And even in the rain

They're there when all I need is time
They're there to give a hug
They're there to bring me chicken soup
When I've a stomach bug

And in return, in thanks for that
And just because they are
I'd do the same for them as well
Whether they're near or far
