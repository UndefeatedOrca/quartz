The tether of my phone feels short
But when I think of it
I haven't got too many things
Preventing short-term split

My texts I can computerize
For calls I fear I'm cooked
But how often I pick up those
Is low the last I looked

So free from chains of telecom
I stride the campus free
And free of nagging, buzzing box
I more the world see
