These poems can give you a slice
Of my life which I think is quite nice
But the people have spake
And they say they won't take
One more where the topic is ice

So in spite I compose this for you
To show what I can and can't do
But I fear that I've felt
That it's starting to melt
So soon topics I'll think of anew
