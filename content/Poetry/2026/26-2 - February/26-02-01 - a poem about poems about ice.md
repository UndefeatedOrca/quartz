Surrounding me on every side
Is ice and ice and ice
It fills my thoughts in every day
Some good and some not nice

My poetry's distracted too
It's all I write about
Which isn't optimal I guess
Not something I would tout

But we will see if things warm up
And heat once more is felt
For then perhaps my subject will
Be all about ice melt
