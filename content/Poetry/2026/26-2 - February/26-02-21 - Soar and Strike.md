The glide on ice like birds in air
In easy-going flight
Until between a puck, and sticks and blades
Have summoned up a fight

Then pounding go the fists aflame
And off the skates they fly
But unlike birds they fall straight down
As referee arms pry

Then once again the dance begins
The frozen, soaring flow
Until the warring starts again
And to the box you go
