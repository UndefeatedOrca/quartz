Now Anne is someone you'll recall
Long after you have met
She's jolly and enlivened and
Confusing I would bet

Just how is it that she goes on
A talkin all that time
While she might listen sometimes there
Just ain't reason or rhyme

But she's a good one in her heart
Despite her harm to sleep
The goodness she has brought to us
Means she we ought to keep
