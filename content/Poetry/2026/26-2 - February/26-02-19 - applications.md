The apps go out tomorrow and
We'll see just how things go
There's more to do, but here's a start
After my moving slow

Deploy the email to apply
Remember all the parts
Don't you forget to think things through
Or miss a term of art

Address the cover letter right
And don't forget the date
Both on the letter and the page
It's bad if you are late

The earlier the better for
The bulk of all these things
And hopefully the sooner sent
The sooner inbox rings
