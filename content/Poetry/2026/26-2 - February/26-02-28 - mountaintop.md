The stars are shining in the night
Above the valley dim
And contrails flow from up above
To give the sky a trim

It's peaceful on the mountaintop
Just me, the sky, the stars
Its quiet on the mountaintop
Without the sound of cars

My friends are on the mountaintop
We chatter as we do
With confidence that bonds will hold
We know we will be true

There's darkness on the mountaintop
But there's no doubt of dawn
Until then though, I'll go to sleep
Afore the coming yawn
