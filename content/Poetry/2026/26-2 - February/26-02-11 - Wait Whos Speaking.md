Announcements and the plans mismatch
And speakers get confused
The convo lineup makes a shift
So I'm a bit amused

For more than one informed me of
The business author's track
But when the introduction came
I business author lacked

Perhaps next time the OSD
Will have a better year
Without the best laid plans derailed
By murder and by fear
