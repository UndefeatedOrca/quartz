The final day before the start
Of lenten fasting looms
Where we will wait until the day
The Christ rose from a tomb

The ordinary time gives way
To penitential fast
Where we remember and relive
The wanderers of past

For forty days we shall embark
On journey to confess
The path begins to tomorrow to
Go through the wilderness
