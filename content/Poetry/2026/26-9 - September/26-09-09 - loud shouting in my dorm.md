It's rather late at night right no
But noisy it is still
The people in my common room
Are shouting to their fill

I do not know what's going on
I wish that they would stop
But maybe soon they'll calm on down
Then I'll hear volume drop

But as it is, I really don't
Know what I ought to do
Perhaps this will just go away
Ere conflict can ensue
