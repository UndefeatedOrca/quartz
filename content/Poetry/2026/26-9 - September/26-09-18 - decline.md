Pure and absurd my nights become
In storytelling spent
And also Taco Bell I guess
I don't know what it's meant

No more elaboration for today
But also no despair 
For thoughtful recollections left
They turned right into air

My bedtime passed, 
I've moved along
Irrational
And moved to tears by song
