Trouble brews in many pots
That boil slowly still
And some can stay contained, but hot
But others try to spill

To spread their problems all around
To make a bigger mess
And burners often aren't turned off
Turned up, I might confess

But rarely there exists a thing
That careful cleaning can't
Dispel into a bin of bleach
Or deal with drain and slant
