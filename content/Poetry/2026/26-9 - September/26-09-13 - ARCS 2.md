The other day I got a text
Come play a game with me
Two hours will be all it takes
Just come have fun, you'll see

Well after sixty minutes passed
The play had yet to start
Which boded ill for what'd been said
For time it would impart

But after four new decks of cards
And three new types of dice
Were finally in motion, well
The game was pretty nice

Sadly though, the game was stopped
Before it was complete
For curfew was upon us and
With that, we can't compete
