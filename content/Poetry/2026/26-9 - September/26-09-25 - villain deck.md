Despite my lack of board control
Or scary giant man
It seems that hunkering in fear
Can work as quite the plan

For villains hold a couple spells
That can keep you alive
Until you go to swing with all
That will then let you thrive

So while I barely won the game
(one life and I'd be dead)
With mercy I contrived a way
To come out all ahead
