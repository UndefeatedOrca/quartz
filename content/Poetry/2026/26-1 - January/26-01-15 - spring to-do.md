The list is growing day by day
Of work I'll need to do
And books with chapters I will read
If I can catch a clue

The stacks of pages pile up
The books I'll need to rent
And after that I fear that I'll
Have all book dollars spent

The papers don't look very hard
But even then we'll see
The way on out is through I 'spose
And what will be will be
