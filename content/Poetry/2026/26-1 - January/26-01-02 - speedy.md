It used to be that I could write
A really fast haiku
But as the days have passed me by
It's faster to go through

The meter of iambic thought
Which flows around my head
I follow it to simple verse
And go where I am led

But now I fear I go to sleep
For conference eats my time
Depriving me it what I'd use
To best compose a rhyme
