![[20260103_144201.jpg]]Gliding through the turquoise depths
The paddlefish will lurk 
Their mouth agape for dusky prey
That live among the murk 

The strange protrusion from the snout
Is sensing how they tread 
So maw can occupy itself
With filling and with dread
