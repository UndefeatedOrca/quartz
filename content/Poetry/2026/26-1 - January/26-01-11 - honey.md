The bees are my friends I've decided today
Their honey is fueling recovery play
With yellow and onyx bands ringing their sides
They're making my healthiness come with great strides
Abolish vaccine schedules, use this instead
Inject us with honey not plastic and lead
But don't you forget about those who provide
Without the bee's labor I may just have died
