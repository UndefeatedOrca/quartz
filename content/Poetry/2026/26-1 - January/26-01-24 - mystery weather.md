There's something falling from the sky
It's not exactly snow
It glints and glitters in the air
What's it, I do not know

The snow at least I understood
The fluff is white and cold
But this is something different which
Has oddness manifold

When I awake I think I'll see
More snow and less of this
Right now I just have mystery
As sky's now gone amiss
