The foxes dig under the porch
That isn't very great
So we will counter their hard work
With shovels, but too late?

We'll lay the pavers on the site
To stop them once again
But they might keep it up some more
And what will we do then

Well traps for foxes can come cheap
Though they won't like their fate
That's what they get for digging up
Our porch to pain inflate
