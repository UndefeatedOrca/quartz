Too powerful for one to wield
Too dangerous to keep
Too tough to break through normal means
Returned from missing deeps

Destroy it where it once was forged
Destroy it once for all
Destroy ere the darkness cast
Expands its ghastly pall

There's danger passing mountain peaks
There's danger in the dark
There's danger in Lothlorien
Though that's also a lark

The enemy is watching you
The enemy has wraiths
The enemy will try you to
The breaking of the faith

But though the darkness deepens now
But though the kings decline
But though the stewards wait for death
There's hope for thee and thine

For heroes brave of Middle Earth
Go out to seek its life
Go out to stop the dark lord's pain
Go out to end the dark lord's reign
Go out so fighting's not in vain
Go out to end the strife
