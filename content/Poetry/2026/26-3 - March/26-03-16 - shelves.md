I've got them stacked up on the walls
They've fully filled the shelf
There isn't space to add up more
Nor even space for elf

The LEGO sits on corners but
Where else could that stuff go?
And back school there's still more
When that comes back: oh no!

There isn't space for shelves up here
I don't know what to do
I can't exactly give books up
When I have read so few
