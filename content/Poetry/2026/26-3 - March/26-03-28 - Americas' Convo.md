The MK Convo's are complete
They're done until next year
I helped to make this final time
End with a worthy cheer

The decorations went up great
The streamers, tables, clothes
The ribbons all across the chairs
And pom-poms in the rows

And music was another thing
That executed well
The beat was kept, the lyrics sung
The tunes were rather swell

Presenters did their part to make
The day a special one
I doubt that any left unmoved
When all of them were done

Not only that, but also food
As tasty as can be
Was there to finish out
America's party

The broom was sacrificed to break
Pinata's solid shell
But what exactly happened there
I don't think I should tell

And overall I hope we taught
With voices high and low
The presence of God's mighty hand
In 'merica's convo
