We've made it all the way to March
St Patrick's Day is soon
The Ides of March are coming up
And warmth again at noon

But Winter won't release its grasp
Without continued fight
With chilly weather clutches' still
Continue in their blight
And daylight savings coming soon
To modify our light

But marching on we go despite
The obstacles involved
To make a future of our own
With all the problems solved
