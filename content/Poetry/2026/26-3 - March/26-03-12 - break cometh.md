The break is breaking on us soon
How did it come so fast
When it is here, then do you think
That it will seem to last

Will spring return in majesty
Or shall the snow extend
The stay that it has just resumed
To fight the heat, contend

Will travel go without a hitch
And traffic absent be
Or will we parked in standstill lanes
Be trapped far as we see

Will friends make showings as they go
On further journeys North
As they go on their pilgrimage
And similarly forth

Will textbooks be a guiding light
I really hope they're not
Ideally there'll be more than do
Than just get lost in thought
