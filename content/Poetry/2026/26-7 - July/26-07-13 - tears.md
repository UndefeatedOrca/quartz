My mother made me cry today
While she was making rice
My mother made me cry today
It wasn't very nice

My mother made me cry today
And I wept ample tears
My mother made me cry today
Confirming all my fears

My mother made me cry today
With mercy not a ton
My mother made me cry today
And made me chop onions
