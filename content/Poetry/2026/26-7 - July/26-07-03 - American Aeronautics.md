They're roaring in the skies above
They're screaming through the air
They're dancing through the airy heights
With just a little flair

They're riding zephyrs on a beast
With metal wings unfurled
And conquering the winds of fate
With dives and rolls and twirls

Their dance of death, for show right now
Brings evil to its knees
But what would run from such a sight
Will not have time to flee

The flying blades, designed for war
Are gleaming in suns light
They're rising up to Icarus
Then diving for the fight
