The clock is ticking far too fast
And time is winding down
Until we get onto a bus
And head on into town

To shout until the gavel's strike
For voting up or down
To see if we can pass a law
Or go home with a frown 

But as for me, I've been wiped out
My thoughts are buried deep
In hibernation, waiting for
My wake to turn to sleep
