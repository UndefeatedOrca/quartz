Mirror, mirror, on the wall
Who's the fairest of them all?
Mirror, mirror, is it true?
The fairest one of all is you?
Mirror, mirror, lead me right
Do you think of me at night?
Mirror, mirror, yes, I know
To the church you cannot go
Mirror, mirror even thus
Is there one to marry us?
Mirror, mirror stop right now
You've been different after vows
Mirror, mirror let me out
It's my turn to go about
Mirror, mirror that is I
Trapped here by a demon sly
Doomed to rot until I die
\---
Mirror, mirror listen close
Tis the trump of he who rose
Mirror, mirror do repent
Ere your time on earth is spent
\---
Mirror, mirror you are free
For you put your faith in Me
