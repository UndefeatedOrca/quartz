Sitting in reflective pool
Waiting for the day to cool
Or for passing fish in school

Hungry yes, but patient too
Never makes a great todo
Until the jaws go snap! ooh-woo

Decisive in a plodding way
The predator prepares for prey
Til setting sun's last final ray
