Although I just got to my home
I'm flying out again
To see what Florida contains
Who knows what's coming then

The Chesterton Society
Will be my short-lived home
Until I hop back on a plane
To once more old haunts roam

I'm not sure if the Instagram
Will stay updated or
I'll keep this trip much quieter
Than one in recent yore

But either way, I soon must go
To sleep and then away
To catch my flight ON TIME I hope
And hopefully both ways
