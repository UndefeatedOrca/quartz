The car is falling into bits 
We're on the highway stopped
Attempting fixes while careful 
To not have head be chopped

The bottom is not staying on 
We used some packing tape 
To keep it stuck into its place
As we go on our traipse

Unfortunately it unstuck
On shoulder we now are
And what will fix it I don't know
Oh dear for failing car 

We'll make it was to Target now
And get duck tape today
But cars we'll switch tomorrow and
For that I'm glad to say
