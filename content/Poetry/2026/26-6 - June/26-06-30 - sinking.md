The air exhaled in bubbles fast
Departing from my lung
To reach into the atmosphere
And go places far flung

But meanwhile I am sinking now
A slow descent to meet
The bottom of the pool where I
Have typically put feet

Surrounded by the water blue
I lay back on the ground
Until I'm gasping up for air
And hear my heartrate pound
