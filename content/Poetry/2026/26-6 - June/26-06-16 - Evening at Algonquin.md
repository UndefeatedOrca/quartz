The night is quiet as a mouse
Though crickets do disturb
No vehicles will gravel crunch
As darkness noises curbs

And yet the distance holds as more
A distant wild call
From wolves or other creatures though
That knowledge can't recall

A goose is honking softly too 
Under the pale moon's light
Providing plaintive undertone
To lovely summer's night
