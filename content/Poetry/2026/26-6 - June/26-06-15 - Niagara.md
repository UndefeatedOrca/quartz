The maple leaf is flying high
Above Niagara Falls
Where water's pouring with noise 
Of many-tongued calls

With power and with majesty 
The falls the volume make
And even far beyond the drop 
Their water still can slake

Descending from the sky above
We feel the gentle mist 
Of what has been a lovely trip
And checks on bucket list
