Completing call of duty's work
Up to the final hour
To do what must be done with all
Of a man's earthly power

His country put before his life 
His job a deadly one 
His comrades bond by sacred oath 
Until the job is done

Forget ye not their bravery 
And cherish up their name
Let not the light of love go out
For worthy of acclaim
