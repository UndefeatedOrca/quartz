We pray for lost and broken souls 
The searching to be found
We pray for gospel message sent
To all the world round

We work to go to ends of earth
To baptize and to preach 
Declare the love of Jesus Christ
And his commandments teach

We dream of nations turned to God
Disciples made of kings 
So every ear has heard the Word
Which every mouth will sing

We know the victory is won 
Was finished on the cross 
And we the triumph march on in 
To tell death of its loss 

We wait the day when faith is sight 
And all that is will be made right
We'll be within glorious light
And all will ever be delight
For death is gone and so is blight 
And nevermore will there be night
We'll glory in the Son
