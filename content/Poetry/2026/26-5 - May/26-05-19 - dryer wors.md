My laundry didn't dry today
I'm worried just a bit
For if it isn't done real soon
To sleep I cannot quit 

I'll have to stay up hours more
Or maybe I just won't
And I'll just not have any clothes
Like hearing that? I don't

So now I simply hope and pray
Success in cycles now
Before my skating tired legs
Can go and to rest bow
