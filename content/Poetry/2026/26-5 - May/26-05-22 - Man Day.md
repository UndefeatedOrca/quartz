It's spitting on the minor dunes 
There's drizzle in the sky
But that will not contain the men
Who from the houses fly

To contest and to fellowship
With wisdom second to
Assessment of the rule of cool 
And deeds of derring-do

Despite the weather men will fight 
And play some games as well
The ground though thorny and quite wet 
Will not a veto tell
