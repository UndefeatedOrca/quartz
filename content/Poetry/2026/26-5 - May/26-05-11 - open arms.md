Release into the arms of God 
Your worries and your cares
Surrender tensions to his arms
And everything that scares

He's gentle savior, meek for us
Yet strong and with a sword
Defender of the weak for us
Storms can with him on board 

Though danger, death, will circle round 
Oh tighter are His arms 
Which opened wide upon the cross
Destroying greatest harm

Now we can sing, we're safe at home
There's nothing left to fear 
Salvation came for all of us 
To wipe out every tear
