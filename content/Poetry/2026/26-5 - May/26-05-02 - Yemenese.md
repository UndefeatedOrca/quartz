The bread is larger than my head
With chewiness to fit
The size which it is brought to us
Added to our meal kit

The dips are hot and tasty too
They're boiling when they're served
We share around the table now
And friendship is conserved

Delight in rice and meat as well
Eyes wide with joyous light
And soup which was brought earlier
Had also brought delight

So tender that the bones fell off
And portion sizes great
Regret is left in just one fact
We won't finish our plate
