Although we cannot make them care
The news is Good so we will share
The trials of the world done 
For victory won by the Son
It's greater than the harshest curse
More beautiful than any verse 
The balm to sinner's aching pain
To spirit's drought, a soothing rain
The falling off of binding chain 
The Mystery, to us made plain
So listen and we shall explain!
