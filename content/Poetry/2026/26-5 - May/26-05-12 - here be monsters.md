The ocean seems to call my name
And ask me what's beyond 
So far away and yet we call 
That vast distance a pond

But in the times and knights and myth
There monsters all we knew
But yet the heroes of the past
Would sail into the blue

What bravery, what heart of steel
Compelled them in their quest
To go where no one'd gone before
And go into the West
