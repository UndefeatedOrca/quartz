The spinners go as ball will roll
The players focus in
They tap the orb with careful shots
Accounting for the spin

And then a wild bounce will score
Withour reason nor rhyme
While careful lined up shots will miss
A good chunk of the time

But I will carry on the fight
For honor of my name
Which holds an etymology
That matches with the game
