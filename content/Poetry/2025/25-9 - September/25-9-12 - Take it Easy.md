It seems that every time I think
It's time to take a rest
A friendly face appears from air
To put that to the test

And suddenly its midnight and
I am not in my home
Instead I work in Montview or
About the campus roam

While problematic it sure is
At least I'm having fun
I'll just take naps in library
So sleep debt will be done
