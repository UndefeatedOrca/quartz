Poured boiling water in the cup
Put teabag in there too
Allow to steep about an hour
And then reverse step two

Now leave your bottle slightly cracked
And wait out half the day
Remember that you made the tea
Expect the heat to stay

Five hours gone but warmth remains
The insulation holds
Imagine if you'd took a drink
Before the timed unfold
