I guess the couplets took a break 
And left the triplets in their wake. 

Perhaps they’re warping in their ways, 
Evolving to create amaze. 

I cannot say exactly though, 
For I lack things I want to know. 

And ere the week is fin’lly done, 
I hope to find you a name
