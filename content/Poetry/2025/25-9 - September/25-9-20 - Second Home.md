The library is where I live 
Which can be a concern
I sit in every corner both
And survey every turn

On second floor is my repose
Except for when it's not
Some days I need the terrace or
A field trip to the rot

The hidden basement for the kids
Computer lab as well
Are options in my time of need 
Of third floor I won't tell

The study rooms are also nice
Depending on the day
Or who exactly's there with me
Or if I've time to play

There's pizzas, pretzels, bagels too 
And shakes where once was yes
Which though depressing, in the end
Is how it has to be 

I know a good bit of the staff
I love the balconies
And if wasn't fully clear
I love the library
