I read a lot of book today
Tomorrow I'll be free
But once I'm done, what will I do
I guess we'll wait and see

The series kind of fell off hard
I struggle now to care
About the how most of characters
Will ultimately fare

But there's still fighters in that world
Who have my sympathy
I want to see them fight and live
For honor and glory

'Cause otherwise I'm mostly done
The secrets were revealed
And if I'm being honest now
They might be best concealed

But someone out there's glad they read
This book while I complain
And all the best to those readers
I'll let their joy remain
