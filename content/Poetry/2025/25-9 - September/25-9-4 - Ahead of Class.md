I'm getting pretty far ahead
In school's work this week
But now I don't know what to do
Adventures do I seek

Perhaps the answer lies in more
Advanced projective thought
So I just reach into week five
And that might have a lot

Or maybe I in hubris think
Something that isn't true
And I'll regret the state I'm in
Within a week or two

But looking at the list I've got
It's looking rather light
So maybe time is in the day
To watch a hockey fight
