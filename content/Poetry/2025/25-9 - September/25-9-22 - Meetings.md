I walk around the campus lawn
And sit in varied chairs
To hunt among a diverse set
And see some other lairs

The moving through my college home
Will take me to dead ends
But frequently the real result
Is running into friends

With just a hint of where you'll be
I wander here and there
And sometimes in the library
Or on a random stair

I say hello by happenstance
Or maybe stay to chat
A schedule might be easier
But who has time for that
