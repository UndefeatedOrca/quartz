The week begins anew today
It's time to rise and grind
Who knows what times will come real soon
What mysteries we'll find

But what I know is that we'll go
Together through the breach
To learn the secrets of the world
And to each other teach

We'll climb the highest mountaintop
Or monogram at least
We'll slay five dozen lanternflies
If we can't find a beast

We'll sail the high and stormy seas
Or settle for the the lake
We'll dine on Michelin starred meals
Or go for a milkshake

The grimoires may be checked out but
If you know where to look
There's arcane knowledge to be found
In your dusty textbook

And if excitement's not your style 
The you kick back and chill
Give patronage to journeyers
And maybe grab our bill?
