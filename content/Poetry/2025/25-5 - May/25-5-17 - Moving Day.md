We've made it to the final day
I post to Roeh's platform
We're moving to a different site
Which you all now may swarm

I think polemics, while quite nice
Don't mix with poetry
And so I'm splitting up the lists
As you should clearly see

I'll keep the rambles, books, and poems
On this, a separate list
And then I won't need to contort
Substack into a twist

I put the first month's archive up
But then I got quite bored
So if you want to read those poems
On Instagram they're stored

I also have them saved as well
I may still post them all
In one big slew, or themed bundles
Not on their lonesome small
