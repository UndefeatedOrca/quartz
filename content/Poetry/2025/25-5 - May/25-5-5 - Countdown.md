I've made it to those final hours
Of time in second year
And I would say that Liberty's
A place that I'd call dear

But homeward bound the wagons go
Back to my family
Whom I do love a lot to see
And will again daily

I'd write a dozen verses here
To speak on all I know
But it is getting rather late
So now to bed I go
