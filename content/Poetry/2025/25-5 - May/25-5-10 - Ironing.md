I did something I haven't done
In really quite a while
I took my clothes and ironed them
That might increase my style

Amazed I saw the creases flee
I don't know where they went
My wardrobe looked so different for
The little time I'd spent

I think the moral of the tale
May be that I should care
More for the clothes that I hang up
And for the clothes I wear
