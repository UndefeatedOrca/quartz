Though some of you are new to this
These have been going strong
For nearly an entire year
Which now seems awful long

On Monday I'll return to where
I worked when starting out
But hopefully the topics will
Be different this time out

To be specific, I sure hope
That AC operates
So that I don't relive last year
Or home work space create

But we will see with morrow's light
Exactly how things go
And if there's something to report
You'll be the first to know
