It isn't every day you come
And take away a win
From those who know the way to play
While you're on your third spin

But on this day we pulled it through
We won our Euchre game
Although we were seven points down
The victory still came

You'd fairly say twas carded luck
Though skill was still involved
But we still made it through the game
Which favorably resolved
### Note
Originally published as Euker, because I don't know how to spell the names of Midwestern card games
