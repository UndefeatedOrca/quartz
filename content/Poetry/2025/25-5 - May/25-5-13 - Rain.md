Drizzle drizzle drip
Time will start to slip
Sprinkle sprinkle sploosh
As the day goes whoosh

You don't see the sun
Then the day is done
All we have is rain
As on the plains in Spain

It doesn't seem to stop
As rain gauge fills to top
The rain is here to stay
At least another day
But it will be okay
