Its been a couple years respite
But I was back once more
To go to where they have sharp tools
And chemicals galore

To where they scrape and scratch and slide
To where the X-rays whir
To where the vacuums and the tubes
The chairs and tools purr

To where the surgeries take place
And cans of air had hissed
That's right I'm fin'lly back again
And went to the dentist
