I hear the tolling chimes tonight
And wonder at the sound
It seems like just a flash ago
Before the world turned round

But now it's time to sleep again
And I've filled in a day
With plenty of activity
And yet not much to say

I guess the only thing to do
I once more go to sleep
And cherish up these days I have
For those are what you keep
