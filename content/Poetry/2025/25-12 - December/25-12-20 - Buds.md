My earbuds lost have now been found
A couple days ago
I got the call that they'd been seen
Amidst the item flow

I'd lost them in the theater
Where I saw Running Man
And couldn't find them in the house
Despite a searching plan

And so I had gone back to school
Without my sound device
Which wasn't bad as I had thought
It sort of turned out nice

But back to earlier this week
After I got the call
I went down to the Cinemark
To see what would befall

And after checking at the desk
The manager was brought
He look around behind the door
But searching was for nought

Just as I went to leave again
The call came out: "they're here"
For in a drawer a sticky note
Had made them disappear

But then I saw my damaged 'buds
Their case cracked in the gears
And dashing all my recent hopes
Confirming all my fears

They'd gotten in recliner's path
A hole was in the front
But thankfully the battery
Had not endured the brunt

So everything still seems to work
There simply is a hole
Which I have patched with shiny tape
To hide damage is goal

The sound remains quite good so I
Will keep them on for time
But someday soon a day will come
When they no longer chime
