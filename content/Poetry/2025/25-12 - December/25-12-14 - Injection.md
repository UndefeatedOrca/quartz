Despite utility there's flaws
Inside the AI bot
Which if you know the tools to use
Can help you out a lot

Inject a prompt in documents
And watch the tricks go live
Where once there was an easy win
You'll now find need to strive

Don't paste my stuff into the chat
Expecting functions quick
Cause I prepared for those like you
I'm sorry, you're not slick
