I made an egg today, twas fun
Went to the grocery store
Then poked around the server files
Confusion? Slightly more

I finished up my final thing
I'm done with school now
So break's officially begun
I made it through somehow

Compiled up a giant list
Of goals to soon complete
But before then I think I might
Cook up more things to eat

Is what I'd say with lesser lists
But this one has a goal
Of cooking up some food as well
Success comes in a bowl

Unless I use a plate instead
But I would count that too
The spirit of the law's enough
To count a checkbox true
