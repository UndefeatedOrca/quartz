Despite the fun I'm having with
A server host right here
My hardware isn't quite brand new
At least that's what I fear

And though the novelty is great
Reliability
Is not what I'd expect from it
Not always what I see

Sometimes it seems to disconnect
For reasons I don't know
Which isn't what I want to see
In any real workflow
