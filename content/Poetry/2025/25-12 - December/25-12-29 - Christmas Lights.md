The brilliant lights go with the sounds
Placed on the radio
So we can celebrate this time
When joyful tidings flow

The strings of luminescent bulbs
Consume the simple walls
Projectors fill the nighttime air
Trees sag with glowing balls

The lights of Christmas flood the night
Destroy dark in their path
I shudder to consider how
Electric bill will math

But though their light will shine a bit
A brighter once came
So blind can see, and deaf can hear
And walk again will lame

So on this chilly winter's night
The season moves along
The world is lit love's pure light
So celebrate with song
