I made my way outside today
To breathe in some fresh air
Before I made my way indoors
And sat back in my lair

I got the analytics up
Set up my holidays
Read some of my most recent book
And read about a maze

Next up might just be radio
Or maybe I'll go out
And see what hijinks I can find
Around and hereabouts

But as it is I think I need
To breathe some more fresh air
Before you find I've disappeared
And merged into my chair
