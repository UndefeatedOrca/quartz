Things happened sure, but I need sleep
The Minecraft is not good
For getting rest in timely ways
Which I know that I should

My friends are playing with me though
So it feels wrong to stop
However I have reached a point
Where I'm about to drop

So if alarm works as it should
I ought to get some sleep
So future days of Winter break
I have my scheduled sleep
