Somehow I made it through the day
Without making cold calls
Perhaps tomorrow that will change
As boredom starts to fall

But as it is I wrote some blurbs
Got groceries with my mom
Played portal game to fill the time
Which actually was bomb

I set up website stuff some more
I think it's almost done
And went on scheduled call with friends
Which also was quite fun

My father now can walk again
Which also is quite neat
With the tool in the belt
There's so much we'll complete!
