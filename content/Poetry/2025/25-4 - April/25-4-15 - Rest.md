I really need to get more sleep
It's getting kinda bad
But having fun with all my friends
Now that is pretty rad

But while I gain enjoyment then
I lose out on much rest
And that will bite me in the butt
When I will have a test

But until that day does arrive
I guess I'll work and strive
To get the rest I dearly need
And not my sleep deprive
