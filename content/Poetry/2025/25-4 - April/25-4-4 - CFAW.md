Moving along through crowded lines
And up and down the halls
I watch the younger kids descend
Upon the campus walls

To see if they will like it here
And try the school out
And stand inside the library
Perhaps to give a shout

They go to class and convo too
And see all our events
Which only happen when they're here
But that's not relevant
