Two years ago we made a meme
That was an inside joke
Promoting where I was employed
With image and words spoke

It made it to our boss's desk
It seemed he liked the thing
And now it's hit the big time with
The nation's reporting

I looked in at Politico
And saw the meme in frame
It made my day to see it there
And now I feel true fame
