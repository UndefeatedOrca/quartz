The time has gone by in a flash
And now we're near the end
When days are long and classes stop
And home we soon will send

With campus com concluded now
And convo on the outs
The finals are arriving soon
But soon to tears or shouts?

The boxes will be filled again
So we can fill the car
And travel all the way back home
Which isn't super far

We'll say goodbyes to end things off
With friends we've known and loved
And who will soon be graduates
Into the real world shoved

To those who will return next year
We'll also say farewell
Until the year begins next Fall
Or sooner, who can tell?

Those days have not quite yet arrived
But they are coming soon
Before the midst of May is done
Or cycle of the moon
