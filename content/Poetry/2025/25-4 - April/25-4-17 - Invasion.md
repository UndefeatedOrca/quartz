There's something shiny in my hair
There's something shining right in there
It came from sparkling orbs above
That moved with my head's shaking shove
In wake of shocking alien plans
To take us up and give us tans
With high UV spectrum light beams
Which makes you levitate it seems
And shocking masked up performers
Are what did greet us past the doors
Which we had passed without a thought
So innocent into the rot
