The boxes filled with random stuff
Are lined up on the wall
For when I leave in two days time
For the semester fall

But first another day at home
To close the summer out
And then the campus is my home
For me to walk about

Just one last day to see the sights
And get some mac and cheese
Clean out my desk at home and work
And turn in intern keys
