I worked at PILF two years ago
Did help with office tasks
I battled with our printer when
Some people still wore masks

My office had a second desk
We had some extra space
We didn't need all the square feet
With which to make our base

And sometimes people worked from home
So only I was there
But they would try to show up when
I went and payed train fair

My payment was a little sketch
But it was good enough
I got my tax forms from them too
And that was kind of tough

If I could live that time again
I think I would go back
But maybe buy myself more lunch
For funds I do not lack
