Another week is all that's left
And then I will be back
To hit the books and go to class
Assignments to keep track

Just one week more to finish strong
And do what I can do
To check the final boxes and
Get lunch a few times too

The other intern left last week
Perhaps we'll meet again
Perhaps we won't, I just don't know
And surely don't know when

One final week to close things out
One final week to see
Exactly what I can get done
In Washington DC
