Ascending mountains digital
We go above the fog
But sometimes failures come our way
Recorded in the log

We're left undaunted by the pain
The poison plants and thorns
The challenge is a bull to us
We'll take it by the horns

But if the troubles come again
That's what we will expect
The battle isn't just one war
Our task we won't reject

And when the dust has settled on
Destruction that we wreak
You'll find us standing tall atop
That distant far-off peak
