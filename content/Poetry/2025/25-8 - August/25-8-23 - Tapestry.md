I bounce around from group to group
Alighting like a moth
Drawn to the flame of human light
Trying to fit the cloth

A quilt is being made these days
Relationships abound
How can they all be managed and
What true self will be found

Not everyone sees everything
About my life and times
Not everyone subscribes to this
And gets to read my rhymes

Not everyone is studious
That's not a problem though
It only means I stay away
When I'm on a workflow

But what connects the dots I'd say
Is that they're people too
I'm glad they're with me on this ride
We make up me and you!
