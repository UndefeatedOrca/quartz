It used to be we got all hyped
For when the next came out
They built up to a final spree
That we all talked about

Then Endgame ended their hot streak
And lights began to dim
Improvement chance declined with time
And hope is now left slim

But some of them are decent-ish
That's better than the rest
Compared to what is truly good 
It doesn't pass the test

Perhaps they'll pull back it again
But I don't hold out hope
I doubt it but I guess we'll see
Or if it's downward sloped
