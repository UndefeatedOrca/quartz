There's mornings where the bus fills up
And some days it does not
The reason for that fact of life
I've given little thought

Perhaps on Fridays less will ride
But Mondays seem to be
Another time when passengers
Are not filling I see

Or maybe I've just got bad eyes
Or lacking memory
And all that I have claimed today
Is cloaked in falsity
