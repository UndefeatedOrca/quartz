The classes now begin again
I check my schedule out
I list all of the things to do
And there's no pain to shout

I've not looked at the details yet
But things are looking good
The workload can be managed right
If I do as I should

Meanwhile my classes have old friends
Whom I've not chanced to see
Except in passing rot hellos
As awkward as can be

But hopefully this year I'll know
Them better than the last
We'll see, I guess in several months
When then the year is past
