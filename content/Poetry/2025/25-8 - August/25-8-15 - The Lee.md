It's wonderful to see my friends
Relax on campus lawns
Experience the pure sunsets
And wake in later dawns

It's fun to go to my old haunts
And read a paper book
Examine all the people pass
From a high overlook

I'm glad that I've returned to school
Glad I'm back in this place
But sometimes I do need a break
To get a little space

The hectic conversations flow
Like rivers to the sea
But sometimes I will guide myself
To rest inside a lee
