I sometimes cannot comprehend
How people come to be
Their faces blurry in my mind
As they come up to me

Then once they say their name I gasp
As blur will leave my sight
So I will recognize them now
And say hi with delight

But I feel bad for blanking first
Before eureka pop
The failure to perceive them feels
Like such a massive flop
