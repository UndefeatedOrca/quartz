My meals are losing sanity
As I regain my stride
I eat a second dinner here
As I through classes glide

A second lunch is menu too
Depending on the day
But breakfast has been lacking so
There is that much to say

And meanwhile snack appear sometimes
And ice cream shows its face
Although in summer months they closed
Its greatest campus place

I wander into many friends
Somehow they still appear
I fight to get their scheduled time
Or see when they are near

In hallways we all lose our minds
In searching dinosaurs
Or go to get some acai
Where Argo once was poured

We draw some maps in library
Forget a continent
We run off to the marching band
Laugh when our breath is spent

I've not gone bowling but I will
That I can guarantee
And there are still a few more friends
Who I would like to see
