The twenty-eight from kickoff were
A painful thing to see
But what we saw in decline
Would make it up to me

A fumble caught by Liberty
A drive to tie the score
Three seconds left from a new kick
Which ended with a snore

And then the second half we played
Life we wanted to win
Which was a little better than 
The state the first half's in
