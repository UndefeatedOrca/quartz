The late night acts amuse the crowd 
With dance and film and song 
They're hyped up on some fresh caffeine
And stayed up far too long 

With flashing lights and belting noise 
And comic hosts to boot
The only thing it lacked to me 
Was gleaming brass to toot 

But overall twas good to be
Awake so late tonight
With friends enjoy the spectacle
In time without sunlight
