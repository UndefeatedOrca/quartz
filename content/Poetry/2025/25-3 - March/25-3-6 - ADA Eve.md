The days go by with buzz and blur
One meeting after next
The work gets done in fits and starts
As sanity is flexed

Departing early time by time
To catch the next big thing
Debate and work for BUSI class
And then hear JD sing

But now I have a moment short
To channel all that time
And tell you all about my day
In meter and in ryhme

Tomorrow is another day
With travel in a plane
But first we travel in a bus 
To glory or for pain
