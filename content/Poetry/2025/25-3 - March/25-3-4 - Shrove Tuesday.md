It was a waffle dinner day
Before the time to fast
Remembering the bygone days
When butter would not last

We now live in another age
With freezers and the fridge
But some things have remained the same
And not shifted a smidge

So as you sit at Lenten door
Consider Christians past
Who've used the coming forty days
To contemplate and fast
