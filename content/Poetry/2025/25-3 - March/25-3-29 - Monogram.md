We rest upon the highest slopes
Beholding from the trees
The distant city streets below
And all there is to see

The campus with its red and blue
And parking deck in white
And natatorium as well
Glows boldly in our sight

The painted rocks of giant disk
Arrayed so close below
Contrast the far off logos that
To our eyes barely show

Beyond the campus cars go by
Careening down Ward's road
And further still the lights are on
And CVCC showed

But what I think is best of all
Of hiking monogram
Is spending time with those I love
And being with the fam
