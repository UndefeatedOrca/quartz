I went into the church today
And yanked upon the rope
To sound the bells with great aplomb
And magnitude and scope

The ringing goes out through the air
And through the churchyard gate
And tells the City of Fairfax
If we have ended late

It'd say if we were done with time
But hasn't done so yet
I fear the dream of that remains
A rather distant bet
