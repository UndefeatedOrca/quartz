I read a book by Heinlein once
About a Martian man
Who made his way to green marble 
And came up with a plan

To fill the world with harmony
And love and joy and peace
He starts a sex cult and what then?
He's shot by the police

And all I want to know is why
The Twitter AI's name
Is the same term for Martian love
Yes sir, that word's the same
---
