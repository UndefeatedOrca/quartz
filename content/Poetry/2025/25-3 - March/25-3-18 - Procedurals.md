I think it's time I had less fun
Basking in freedom's glow
Because sometimes I fear I may
Give nonsense a new go

Be emails or be fonts around
The zany will go on
I feel I may be on a roll
Approaching this new dawn

But as I live my life I guess
I'll live another day
Because I will move on to go
Make history someday
