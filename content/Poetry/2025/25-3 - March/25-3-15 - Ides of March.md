Don't go into work today
It will not go well
The Senate has a bone to pick
If you couldn't tell

You'll be dead and gone today
If you go out there
They're doubting all the power you have
If you even care

Ides of March are on this day
Down in history
When Caesar stabbed to death went down
It was quite gory

Don't forget what was today
Caesar had no fun
"Et tu, Brute" you heard him shout
But what's done was done
---
The perspective switch in the middle might be weird, but I wasn’t sure what else I could really do there, other than write two poems.

Also, I tried using a slightly different meter for this one, not entirely sure it worked, but it was something new and I went for it.
