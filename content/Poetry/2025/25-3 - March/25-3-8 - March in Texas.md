I think I might move from my home
To reach a temperate clime
Where weather gives a T-shirt vibe
No matter what the time

The day we've had in Texas has
Reminded me of heat
Which I've not felt for months on end
It really can't be beat

So while the Summer months are far
I'll take this distant spot
And praise it for the balmy air
And for the fact it's hot
