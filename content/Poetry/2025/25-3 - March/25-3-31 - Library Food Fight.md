The flying condiments began
The salvo of the war
So say bad things about my life
But don't say it's a bore

I lived in peace a happiness
Until the food did fly
And then I served as Switzerland
And aimed just not to die

Surreal I would describe these times
Spent in a new orbit
Adventures of the food fight kind
Are always worth the bit
