While PowerShell is fun and all
And minecraft is now on
I think tomorrow something new
Will rise alongside dawn

Cause linux is a ton of fun
And hosting things is great
But I have schoolwork to do
It lingers on my plate

So while I could ignore it and
Host ebook stacks instead
The writing on the wall is clear
And now it must be read

So VPN, to wayside go
And YouTube downloader
I won't be building websites yet
To schoolwork I spur

Goodbye Portainer, Docker too
I'll see you all again
The Silksong speedrun attempt two
Will go to future then

And back to powerpoint it is
Despite what I might want
I'd rather get the project done
Than bad grades transcript haunt
