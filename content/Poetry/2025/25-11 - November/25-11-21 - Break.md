I've made it all the way to home
Thanks to some awesome friends
And now we wait to see how I
Will choose to break time spend

I have a paper that I'll do
Another that I'll start
Perhaps I'll finish up my book
It is fantastic art

But otherwise my schedule's blank
Except for just one meal
Thanksgiving family dinner
Will sure give joy to feel
