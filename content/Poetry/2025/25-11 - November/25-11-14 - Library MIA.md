I took a little bit of time
In morning study room
But other than that precious hour
Today I'm on the zoom

I went to convo, then the rot
Went to SOBUSI next
When summoned by a friend in need
By a delivered text

And then a Southward journey out
To visit for the first
The building that I have discussed
(my readers are well versed)

Beyond that time I went to North
And got a minty shake
Alongside meal of chicken, fries
Which I on road did take

To meet the Meeters at the rot
And then some games to play
Until the I got a pleading text
To boredom go and slay

So somehow then I bowling went
And played so terribly
That though I was the only strike
I barely broke fifty

To close the night I played some Skong
The platforms baked my soul
But other than that little hitch
We might be on a roll

But to return to subject now
I didn't read my book
And though I did spend time in it
Library time was took

By journeys all across the land
Which I thought rather fun
Though with the curfew hour here
I think my day is done
