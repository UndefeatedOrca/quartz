created: 2025-07-04

Explosions break out overhead
In red and green and gold
They burst into their vibrant shapes
That just a moment hold

The blue and purple shatter skies
The silver streamers flow
As smoke fills air across the land
And high explosives blow

The sky was made of brilliant light
Despite the lack of sun
And ne'er say July the Fourth
Is ever lacking fun
