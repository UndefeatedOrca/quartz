It isn't often that I see
The bus be scheduled right
But often when mistakes are made
They make me want to fight

Although that will remain the norm
Exception was today
When sign said that the bus was gone
And to expect delay

But lo, behold, the bus arrived
Just as the schedule said
Despite the absence on the sign
Which we in line had read

I won't complain about this one
Today I will be glad
Perhaps the trend will go on more
So I'll never be sad
