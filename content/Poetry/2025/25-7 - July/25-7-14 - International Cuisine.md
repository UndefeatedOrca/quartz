I built a three chairs at work today
With cushions and five wheels
And for my labors I got food
A chocolate and lunch meal

The chocolate came from 'cross the sea
Reactionary France
And you should try confections too
If you're given the chance

Italian also I consumed
Their garden is delish
Alfredo, chicken, broccoli
Create a tasty dish

The international array
Past that did not extend
My dinner came from in the fridge
And with that, tale's at end
