It took three weeks of reading but
I finally found the thing
That linked up all the pieces and
Allowed my thoughts to ring

We'll see what comes about from this
I need a bit of time
I feel the sources syncing up
They're starting to make rhymes

But that will wait for morrow's dawn
Tonight I go to sleep
With hope that when I do awake
I will, eureka, keep
