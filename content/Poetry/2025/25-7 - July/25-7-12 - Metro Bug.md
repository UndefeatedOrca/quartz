The creepy crawlies move along
Who knows just what they are
The only thing I know for sure
One's in my metro car

It's moving toward my foot it seems
That is its target now
Although I've moved my foot a lot
It still will follow, wow!

It sits beneath my upraised heel
I could just squash it flat
But no, I'll let it linger there
Until it's bored of that

It goes off to another guy
And climbs upon his heel
But when he moves his foot, I find
My greatest fear is real

The bug can jump from several feet
And now it's back to me
But soon enough it leaps away
Another thing to see
