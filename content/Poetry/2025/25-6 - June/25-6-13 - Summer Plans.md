It's been a long time since I've heard
From several of my friends
So when my time on road is done
Some minutes I will lend

To catch up with that motley crew
Of students and of staff
Where we can once again relate
Again to chat and laugh

It's been too long to justify
But even so I'll try
To bring to fore connections old
If only to say hi
