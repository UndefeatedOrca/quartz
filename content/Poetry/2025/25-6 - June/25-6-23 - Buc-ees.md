A Buc-ees is a lovely place
With lots of food and fun
And drink machines and fresh made food
It might be over done

But over all I'm glad they're there
To give consistent stop
Though home decor does seem to me
A bit over the top

But though they have a lot of stuff
We know it won't be said
That Buc-ees stocked our only need
For they did not have bread
