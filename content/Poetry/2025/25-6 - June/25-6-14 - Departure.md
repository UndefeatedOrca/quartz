Although I will depart quite soon
It doesn't feel like it
And yet the clothes are packed away
And we leave in a bit

I haven't packed a second bag
I'll do that in the morn
I think I may regret that choice
Which now, I've myself warned

But even though the plan is bad
I want to get some sleep
And letters need to be written
But then my rest I'll keep
