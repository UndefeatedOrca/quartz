A thwack or two rebounds the ball
It slices through the air
And bounces on the edge of tar
We barely call it fair

The net it sliced right over, just
Speeding to hit the court
Say what you will about old men
But they can choose a sport

When darkness falls we still play on
Straight through the failing light
Until the shadow silhouettes
Are all we see in night
