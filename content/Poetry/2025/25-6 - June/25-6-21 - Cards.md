The shuffled decks go in to play
To draw and play and curse
As what began as a bad hands
Draws in to one that's worse

There's not a pair that can be used
Nay not one to be found
But maybe fortunes will improve
When there's another round

Another deal declares a joy
We can begin to play
And not get stuck with ninety points
Without touching the fray

The points though piled high and tall
Are matched by those around
And when the game will end at last
Will victory be found?

Perhaps in some games you may win
In others lose as such
But when you're playing with your fam
It matters not so much
