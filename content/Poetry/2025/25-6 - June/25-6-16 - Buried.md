In legislature's vaunted halls
Composed of solid stone
Men lie encased inside the walls
In caskets all alone

The man who built the capitol
The architect's own tomb
He lies in silence ne'er disturbed
Until the final doom

The world will go around the sun
Elections come each fall
But he won't hear a single thing
While resting in that wall
