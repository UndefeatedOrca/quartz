We're going home today at last
It's been a real long way
But it's not over yet so wait
To "yay, it's over" say

We've got one day to finish up
The final leg at last
And then we'll look back at the weeks
That on the road have past

But now it's late, I need some sleep
I've stayed up rather late
To catch a moment with a friend
That I appreciate

And we will stop to say hello
To a few more of those
The friends I've missed these past few months
So we'll see how it goes
