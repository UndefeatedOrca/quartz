I've had a couple days to rest
So now it's time to go
Return to work and hit the grind
No longer take it slow

They changed the buses yesterday
So I will now adjust
To find out how the system works
As I'm, toward DC, bussed

The times are new and so's the route
Or maybe it's the same
Perhaps I'm making a big deal
Out of something that's lame

But we will see; tomorrow comes
And if I do get lost
At least I'll write about the time
And how much sane it cost
