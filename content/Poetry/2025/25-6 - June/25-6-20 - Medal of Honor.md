Red, white, and blue upon the flag
That many men have served
With honor, glory, valor too
Commending is deserved

For those who go beyond the call
They are remembered dear
For those who are courageous in
The smirking face of fear

To celebrate the warriors
When thrown into the worst
The Congress made a medal to
Commend the very first

The Medal of Honor is the peak
Of commendations height
Those who have received its weight
Have fought a greater fight
