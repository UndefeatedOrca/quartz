I have a hunch, don't ask me why
That all will be okay
There's joy in life and work and school
In toil and pause and play

The people that you meet in life
Will help you more to see
The good existing all around
And all the near beauty

The friends you make will shape your path
So I'm glad I chose well
Though if I chose or they chose me
I'm not sure we can tell

So as you go about your day
Remember the great gift
For friends are worth their weight in gold
And that your heart should lift
