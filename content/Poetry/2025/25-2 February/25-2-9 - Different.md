The world is not the same today
As it was yesterday
A thousand little things were tweaked 
While some did static stay 

What does it mean to change the world 
What does it mean to you? 
Does it mean standing for what's right
And saying what is true? 

Who knows what tides of fate portend
Who knows the future now
Just do the next right thing I guess
And muddle on somehow
