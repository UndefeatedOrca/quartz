With eagle's eyes I watch the world
Intent on those I see
Across a hundred feet or more
You cannot hide from me

A moment and I've got the snap
The snipe has been complete
Then I return to living life
Perhaps get food to eat

The art of spotting is complex
A careful survey looks
To spot the features of your friends
As they read their textbooks

But once you know the tricks of trade
A sniper you will be
And if you train for decades more
You might even get me
