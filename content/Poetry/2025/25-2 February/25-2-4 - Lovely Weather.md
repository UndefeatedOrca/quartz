As warm sun rays descend on us
They warm the air and sky
They drench the ground with brilliant light
The heat comes from on high

The seasons turn about their wheel
But sometimes they will trip
And I can capture Springs new heat
As Winter starts to slip

So though next week we may get snow
In warmth, today I bask
And ne'er shall I complain about
When Winter fails her task
