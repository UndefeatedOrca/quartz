A tree encased in glassy sheen
Preserved in solid ice
Like mammoths from an ancient age
Immobile in cold vice

With every branch enclosed it stands
Like Winter's mood made real
A stasis holds the plant in place
Its growth always to steal

Perhaps with time the Spring will come
And free a frozen world
Until that time the world is ice
Which from gray clouds was hurled
