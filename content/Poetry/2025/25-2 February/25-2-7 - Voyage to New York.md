The train was nice, by that I mean
I did stuff for debate
And though I have my doubts sometimes
I do not always hate
The work I do to be prepared
And try to be first-rate

But then we made it to New York
We walked the canyon streets
To make our way to the hotel
And then to get our eats
Then finally we hit Times Square
And made the day complete
