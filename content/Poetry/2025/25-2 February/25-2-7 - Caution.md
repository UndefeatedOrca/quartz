The way to win a heart and mind
Is not the same for all you'll find
For when a person's in a bind
Reactions can be undefined

In conversation that you make
A careful tread you ought to take
Still be yourself, no don't be fake
But watch yourself, don't feelings break

In tough talking it's safe to say
That there will never be one way
But on the safe side you should play
Protect the ones you love I pray
