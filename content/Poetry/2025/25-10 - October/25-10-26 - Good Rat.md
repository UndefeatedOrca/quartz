The best of art will make you sit
And ponder on its end
How did the characters collide
How did their efforts spend

Did happiness come well deserved
Were tragedies their right
Was truth the thing that author told
In details large or slight

And most of all, what will result
From living in that world
That with their brilliance and a pen
The author has unfurled
