I've gotten lots of work complete
It doesn't quite make sense
For weeks I've coasted through assigned
Twas thinking I am dense

But now that book is finally done
I locked in like a gate
Demolished essays like a bomb
I've gone to detonate

Now only one thing's in my path
The Lockheed Martin plan
To outline how they'll market things
And how to do that? Man . . . 

If I'd been thinking way back when
I wouldn't have chose it
The lack of varied customers
Annoys me just a bit

But I will overcome the pain
And finish marketing
I'll get back on the school grind
Where I am still speeding
