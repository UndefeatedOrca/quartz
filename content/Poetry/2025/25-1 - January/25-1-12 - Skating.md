Around around around we go
Around the oval ice
Around the children on the wall
Around our skates will slice

Wobble wobble wobble now
Wobble on the turn
Wobble on the straightaways
Wobble fall and burn

Try and try and try again
Try and stay upright
Try and wobble less and less
Try and keep turns tight

Skating skating skating on
Skating til we're done
Skating on the ice with friends
Skating was real fun
