The time has come and gone today
We made it through debate
Activity I maybe(?) love
Despise or maybe hate?

But where's there space for hate this day
Nay naught allows to loath
For every one of us is made
By he who daisies clothes

The joy in life, what fun, what light
The sky, the sea, the land
They take us into joyous heights
Just see the Father's hand
