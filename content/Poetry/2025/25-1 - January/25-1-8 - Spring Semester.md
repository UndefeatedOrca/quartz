The time arrives to make my way
Off to ole Liberty
Where food is made all times of day
For us to take freely

Back to the friends from college years
Back to my lovely dorm
Yet still in my Virginia dear
So it will not be warm

The story that is life goes on
And starts a chapter new
Perhaps cast will shift a bit 
Perhaps twill feature you
