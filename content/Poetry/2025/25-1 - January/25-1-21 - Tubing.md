Inflated rubber plastic hill
Three handles on the side
A push, a kick, you're off the top
You're starting on the ride
Then suddenly the balance shifts
You spin and yet you slide
The hill is at an end and now
Your motion is denied
So ends the first of many runs
Now that tubing you've tried
