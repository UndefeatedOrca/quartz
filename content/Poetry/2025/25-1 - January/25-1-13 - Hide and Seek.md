We gallivant around the floor
We go and check on every door
We look round once then look some more

We hide in crevice, crack, and knoll
We aim to hide, unfound our goal
We see if Josh fits in trash hole

We started out with Poker, then
We played, we sought, we hid again
We rate the game a perfect ten
