I opened up the door to look
I looked to see the storm
But though the flakes were yet to fall
I did see something warm

A fox across the front lawn looked
And saw me looking back
The creature, hasty, moved away
To take another track

But for an instant in the cold
I wondered what I'd do
If in the moment that lone fox
Had tried, to door, get through
