The angels sing their songs above
Divine the melody
While here on earth we hum a tune
That's always found off-key

But even so there's some who sing
A little more in tune
They know someday the Savior comes
And that the day is soon

Their words illume and speech glory
In greatness of above
Their actions show to everyone
The true meaning of love
