There's people on this campus who
I hold quite dear to me
I hope that though I'm indirect
That simple fact they see

Who though they've seen my foibles they
Despite all that will take
A moment or a lunch break to
Say hi for old times sake

The people who I wave to and
Wave back without a trial
The ones who say hello to me
And greet me with a smile

So though I'm not the best at it
Know when push comes to shove
You all are very dear to me
I send you all my love
