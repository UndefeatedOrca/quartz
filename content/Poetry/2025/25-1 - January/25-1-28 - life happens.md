These past few months I've made a poem
Composed one everyday
But there are times that I'm left with
A dearth of things to say

My days are full of things I do
But not all's fit to post
So though my day is full of stuff
For content I am toast

And lacking mental energy
I can't devise anew
A story or a song at night
That can be seen by you

In other news I think I might
Delete the instagram
And shift this to an email list
That might go to your spam

So if you'd want to be on that
I guess I'll hear you out
Just write up a short message and
Reply to give a shout
