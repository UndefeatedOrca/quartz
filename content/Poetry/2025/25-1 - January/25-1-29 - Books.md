The stories from the past arise
From printed paper page
And characters from other worlds
Will stride upon mind's stage

A hero charges to the aid
Of captive beauty Belle
Or journeys all the way into
The fiery pits of hell

For glory, love, and honor too
They make their mark in time
The wonder of the story is
Truly something sublime
