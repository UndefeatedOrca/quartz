The airport is a lovely place
I guess, if you hate life
And want a government to search
To confiscate your knife

For centralized control to run
Your every single stop
And stores to charge you fifteen bucks
For two bottles of pop

Yet even so, the airport is
Our gateway to the sky
And worthy of the suffering
Is just the chance to fly
