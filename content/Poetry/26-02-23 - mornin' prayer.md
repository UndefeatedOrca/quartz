The time of day has come again
When I will need to sleep
If I'm to have a fighting hope
To my appointment keep

The early mornings are a fight
That I intend to win
But making it before the sun
Around the earth will spin

Is difficult I have surmised
A trial I would say
But even so I'll make it there
And early morning pray
