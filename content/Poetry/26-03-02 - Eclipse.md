Eclipse is coming to us all
If you're in the US
But am I bold enough to go
Wake up without the stress

Of setting when alarm will sound
And getting out of bed
When clouds may fill the box of sky
To hide the moon of red

I cannot even tell you where
The moon itself will show
Will mountain block the spectacle
Yea, where then must I go

But maybe still I'll get up and
Examine special sight
It isn't often that you'll see
Something like that at night
