It's beginning to look a lot like CFAW
Everywhere you go  
Take a look at the grand lobby
There's chairs everywhere you see
Prepared for all the parents in their rows
  
It's beginning to look a lot like CFAW  
You know what is in store
And you'll go walking on past vines  
And you'll see the great convo lines
Straight to Commons Four 
  
A biomedicine major  
And fellows to date her  
Is the wish of Julie and Jan  
Dolls that will talk  
And will go for a walk  
Is the hope of Stewart and Ben  
And Mom and Dad can surely wait for college to begin

It's beginning to look a lot like CFAW
Everywhere you go  
There's good food in the dining hall
And portions are never small
You wish that it wasn't all made for show  
  
It's beginning to look a lot like CFAW
Soon your class will start
And a child will steal your seat  
You know can't compete
So you play your part
  
It's beginning to look a lot like CFAW
The high-schoolers are born
But the prettiest sight to see  
Is the exit that will be  
On your Sunday morn  
  
Sure it's CFAW once a-more
