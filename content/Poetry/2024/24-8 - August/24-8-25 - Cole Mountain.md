Up and up and up we go
Up to Cole Mountain's peak
And when we make it to the top
We'll find the view we seek

Up and up and up we went
And then we hit the top
So finding ourselves cresting the hill
Our company could stop

Round and round and round we went
Patrolling its summit
Fantastic views up there are seen
And beauty I'll submit

So if you've got some extra time
I really would suggest
The hike up to Cole Mountain's peak
Upon a day of rest
