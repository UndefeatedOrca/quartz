Do angels paint the sky each day
In different shades of blue
Do every morn they start again
Painting the sky anew

Do angels paint the sky each day
Using a great paintbrush
Is every morn made up unique
From sunrise's first blush

Do angels daily paint the night
And all the stars adorn
Only to cover up their work
Upon the coming morn

Do angels craft the sunrise fair
And color the sunset?
If beauty is what marks their work
Then it is a safe bet
