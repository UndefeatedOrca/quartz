A haunted thicket of strange rules
Control our energy
And when those regs are brought to light
It's not easy to see

The clearing price and auctions too
That work an RTO
Are things that simply aren't too great
In fact they ought to go

As bad as gold-plating can be
At least someone's the boss
And they can take the blame and pain
When power's at a loss
