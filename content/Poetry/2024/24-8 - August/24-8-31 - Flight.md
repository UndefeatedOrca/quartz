Graceful arcing through the air
Under the Montview eaves
Their slender bodies crafted from
Some random paper sheaves

Up and down, around and through
Their bodies float up high
The paper planes tossed in public
Will sometimes go awry

Away and out, too high too low
The fly around the room
The chaos in their joyful wake
Will not permit a gloom
