The man named for a forest sang
And I showed up to hear
Other than that not much happened
At Block Party this year

Throughout the party's music set
Where Mr. Frank did sing
The speakers were turned up so loud
That still my ears do ring

So while I'd surely go again
To night it was a plus
I certainly could go without
Obnoxious tinnitus
