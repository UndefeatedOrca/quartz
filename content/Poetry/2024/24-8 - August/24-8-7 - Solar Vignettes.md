**Stage**
Extending through the overcast
A sunbeam still peaks through 
Illuminating distant lands
Where sunlight still shines true 

**King**
On the horizon there is light
On mountains like a crown
The great corona formed by clouds
And far off sun shined down

**Unveil**
The cloudy sky is shattered as
Horizon's changed by wind
And as sunset broke through the clouds
A quiet watcher grinned

**Night**
The distant moon and further stars
Illuminate the night
And far away on distant hills
The sun falls out of sight
