There's strange men who invade my room
They claims to be RAs
But their habit of not knocking
Has left me in a daze

Like yesterday I'm in my room
Writing my nightly poem
When suddenly a strange man came
To come invade my home

And then again later that night
My quad door swung open 
And in walked my RAs again
At least an hour past ten

They walked and talked around my quad
Twas far past my bedtime 
So for my lack of sleep they get
Tonight's short little rhyme
