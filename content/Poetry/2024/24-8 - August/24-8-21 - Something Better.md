The wisdom of Kermit is something to hear
The knowledge provided with Rowlph over beer

The root of the problem is easy to trace
For come Father's day one's bewitched by a face

And suffer all men do, for nights can be long
But always we hope something new comes along

That thing could be better, that thing could be nice
Perhaps it might be bought at very low price

But until some genius finds a substitute
The only solution is a woman cute

Like men, Kermit suffers in this awful strife
Better won't come along in this here life
