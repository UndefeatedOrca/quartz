I'm not quite sure how we got here
But it has been three hours
And I'm approaching last reserves
Of poker playin' powers

I'm still atop on total chips
But challengers approach
And through the fold and call and raise
They on my lead encroach

But over time my skills improve
And it might be enough
If I can hold a poker face
And manage a good bluff

**later**

A bit past ten we called it quits
'Cause some of us need sleep
And happily I can report
That I, my lead, did keep
