It turns out that our AC works
Just not so very well
But that seems only to matter
When temperatures swell

Seeing as everyone one is still
In their ole office seat
'Cause it turns out that you don't need
AC if there's no heat

So while I'm gone before the fix
It doesn't matter much
And I'll just use my desk for now
In the office as such
