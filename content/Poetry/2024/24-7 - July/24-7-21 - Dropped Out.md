With Biden gone. Harris is in
New battle lines are drawn
And who knows what new mess of things
These new events will spawn

The Democrats whole party is
Awash in disarray
And meanwhile the Republicans
Don't quite know what to say

And so I'll wait for dust to clear
Sit back and watch the show
The thing to do is make popcorn
Who knows how things will go?
