My game was off to a rough start
My luck was rather down
But I could build the longest road
Along an island town

With that I captured two more points
And extended the match
But Mac was just too far ahead
His points I could not catch

So finally the game was done
It ended in a blur
And though I didn't win the urn
I retained my honor
