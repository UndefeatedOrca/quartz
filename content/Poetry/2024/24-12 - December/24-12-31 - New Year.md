Another year begins today
Again the seasons roll
Another set of days to fill
And many miles to stroll

Another year of friends and foes
Remix of last year's days
Another batch of weeks to mark
And watch a passing craze

Another year to grow and change
Repeat the months once more
Another set of hours to watch
And find out what's in store

Another year, another day
A world to till and tame
Another month another hour
And it feels much the same
