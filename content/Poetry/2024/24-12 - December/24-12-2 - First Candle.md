It's virtue when it makes no sense
Defy the odds it must
It says that one might triumph yet
If one can only trust

The doomed and dreary souls will still
In anguish lift it up
The tortured captive clings to it
As pain refills his cup

In darkness it alone is light
Its limits have no scope
Rebellions? They are built on it
Built on the ground of hope
