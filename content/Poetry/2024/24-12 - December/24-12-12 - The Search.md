The wiki has the things I need
If I can search them out
'Cause trawling through hundreds of teams
Is what it's all about

But as for things which none have run
I'll have to make myself
'Cause there's not something out there made
I could pull off the shelf

So time will tell what break will hold
Perhaps I'll have some fun
But on the chance that I get bored
Then real debate gets done
