To see into the distant fog
And go beyond the waves
To battle pirates and their kin
The robbing spaceman knaves

For glory, honor, and the urn
To settle on new land
To build a port or three to trade
And get a better hand

We search the seas for gold and more
For when the fog is gone
We build our roads and cities there
To settle all Catan
