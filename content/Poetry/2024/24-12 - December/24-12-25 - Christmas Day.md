On Christmas day the wait is done
For Advent's passed away
A new season's begun for us
Twelve nights and days to stay

For a few thousand years ago
Quoth angel: "do not fear"
That time was when our God came down
Our hearts to fill with cheer

So Merry Christmas to you all!
To all of you Good Night!
And if it's morning where you are
Then let the sun shine bright!
