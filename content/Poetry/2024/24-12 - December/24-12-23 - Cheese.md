Now we returned some time ago
To house without much food
And lack of cheese had left me in
A somewhat dour mood

When Dad went to the grocery store
He didn't get it still
And so my need for cheese did grow
My need to get my fill

But come today we made the trip
To Costco to get more
And there we found the yellow blocks
Right in that wonder-store
