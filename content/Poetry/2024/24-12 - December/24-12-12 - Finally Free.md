I've made it all the way to break
All my assignments done
It seems that I can take a while
Relax and have some fun

I'll go into the city and
I'll take a walk or two
I'll spend some time just sleepin'
But then what will I do?

Perhaps I'll get out my old bike
And spin around the block
Or maybe I'll write messages
Upon the street in chalk
Or see the friends that I have missed
And catch up with some talk

And then when all is said and done
I will return and rest
With solace in the simple fact
That I have no more tests
