I tell you that there'll be a time
When all will be at rest
And someday all the chains shall break
To free all the oppressed

And even in the darkest night
There always shines a light
For days will come when there's no need
To pick up swords and fight

And on that day we'll sing and praise
Our song shall never cease
For He, the subject of the song,
Is He who brings us peace
