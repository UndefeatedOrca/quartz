I'm going to Chicago soon
To hear a band perform
A band in which I once was part
And marched with in good form

We'll hear a couple other groups
And they might be okay
But Robinson is where it's at
We're there to hear them play

The sightseeing might be fun too
I heard it might be cold
But come what may I'm going there
For that's what I've been told
