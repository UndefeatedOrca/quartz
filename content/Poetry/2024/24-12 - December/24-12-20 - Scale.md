A thousand feet above the ground
I see the snow topped farms
And think about the titan's globe 
That's held in Atlas' arms

The acres and hectares of land
That fill my lofty view 
Comprise a tiny portion of
The land once known as New 
Which Christopher Columbus found
In fourteen-ninety-two

But mores's the land across the sea
In Europe and beyond
And that forgets the sea between
That o so maybe pond

And far below the crust of earth 
There is the molten core 
And far above terrestrial bounds
Are moons and stars galore 

The scale of it defies the mind
How can it have such size?
I can't compute a thousand miles
Or see them with my eyes

So even now as I look out
From aircraft altitude
I ponder brobdingnagian 
I think on magnitude
