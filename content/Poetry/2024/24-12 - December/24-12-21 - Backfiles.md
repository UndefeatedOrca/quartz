The files of old are stored in vaults
Not meant to see the sun
But sometimes I explore the bins
To look and see for fun

It turns out that the work I do
Has oft been done before
The work I have to do this break
Is bring past to the fore

The files are old enough to have
A header made to print
And strategies up at the top
In case you need a hint

They tell of tales of martyrdom
They speak against all theft
They all need me to highlight them
For blocks I'm left bereft

But this stuff is of higher grade
Than some odd args I've cut
So come the Spring I won't allow
The backfile to stay shut
