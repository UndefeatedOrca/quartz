To win the war your budget ought
To fund the Navy's ships
'Cause if they fail you'll find that now
The power balance flips 

For when your subs are surfacing
And ships are in the dock 
You may just find that war will start
And have a nasty shock

To get your troops across the seas
Without a ship you're stuck 
And if you want to move a tank
Without a boat? Good luck!

And as for Army's drones and such 
I think I'd rather not
'Cause if you subsidize bad work
Success rates aren't so hot 

But even if they were to work
Without Navy you're shot 
Cause if the drone's still on mainland
Then all your work's for naught
