The magic of the winter night
Will not remain when day
Yet tokens of the other world
Will bring to mind the play

The world when all's alive and light
The world that's lit by moon
Will always feel, when sunrise comes
That it's ended too soon

But even so the joy will stay
That joy so dearly felt
Will ne'er not once be lost to time
Although the snowmen melt
