I made it to AI club
But first I saw the sun
And when I left AI club
I guess the day was done

I entered in before five
And left the club by six
The sun had suddenly gone out
Perhaps it called in sick?

A week ago this wasn't
A thing that sun did
A week ago the sun stayed
And its face wasn't hid

But then on Sunday something
Just messed up all the clocks
The Sunday something really
Makes mornings kinda rock

But then we get to ev'nings
And that where all fun ends
For when we get to ev'ning
An early dark portends
