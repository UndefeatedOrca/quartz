The New Year starts tomorrow with
The first day of Advent
Which is a time of fasting though
It is different than Lent

It's time to prep your heart for when
The Lord himself comes down
To take the form of helpless babe
Inside a crowded town

Remember what it's like to wait
To see the Savior come
Remember Zechariah who
In waiting was struck dumb

So hold off on your Christmas cheer
And keep Mariah iced
'Cause Advent is the season that's
Before the birth of Christ
