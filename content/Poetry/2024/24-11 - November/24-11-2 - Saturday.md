I spent this lovely Saturday
While working in the rot
To school's variety of tasks
I chose to give my thought

Beyond the tasks of school I had
A bit of time to read
The haunting tales of Father Brown
Where men both die and bleed

Combining that with charting skills
Is not a perfect match
But I will take what I can get
In leisure time I catch
