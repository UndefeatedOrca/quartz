The trees are burning on the hills
Their torch is orange and red
Upon the hills the frozen fire
The greens of Spring have fled

The falling leaves illume the path
In shades of rust and gold
The cycle of the seasons goes
As it has done from old

But soon the leaves will all be gone
And naked be the trees
Preparing for Winter to come
And its oncoming freeze
