I'm thankful for this day we have
The rain and clouds and all
The moon's pale beams and sun's bright light
And this season of Fall

I'm thankful for my friends as well
From work and church and school
I don't know how how you manage to
Put up with this here fool

I'm thankful for my fam'ly too
Siblings and cousins all
And Mom and Dad who while at school
I ought more often call

I'm thankful for my country where
Our people can be free
I'm thankful for the soldiers who
Gave up their lives for me

I'm thankful for the God above
Whose love 'bove all is true
I'm thankful for my readers
And by that I mean you!
