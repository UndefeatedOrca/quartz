And as a bonus poem today
I feel the need to note
That over five entire months
A poem a day I've wrote

And I discovered yesterday
My insta can't contain
The mass of stories posted here
My highlight's filled again!

So now I ask the people if
This is the right format
Or if there's somewhere else to post
Your daily poem with Pat
