We made it back to school at six
After the long bus ride
It was so late it twas early
So rot breakfast we tried

Though sleep deprived we ate our fill
And then I went to class
I somehow made it through that time
But I was low on gas

So when my 8:15 was done
I picked up all my stuff
And trundled up to good old East
To make my sleep enough

Five hours later I awoke
And felt somewhat better
So I prepared to go to class 
And made it out the door

This journey that I have been on
The bumpy twistin' ride
Has finally made it back to
The place that I reside
