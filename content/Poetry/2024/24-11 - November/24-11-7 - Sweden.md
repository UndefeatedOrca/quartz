Miss Indiana Smith would say
Sweden's close to her heart
But to explain that zany tale
I'm not sure where I'd start

The stories on the bus we told
Had some variety
But Sweden was always a place
That would our setting be

Whether to seek a hidden grave
Or find a misplaced cure
Or meet a new exchange student
With all intentions pure

The Swedes welcomed with open arms
The BuslitzoneCU
It is a place where some who live
Make time to schedule you
