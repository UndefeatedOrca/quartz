I went to see a play today
And that's all well and good
But weeks ago I saw Shakespeare
And speak on that I should

The play was called As You Like It
And it was not a bore
For it was Shakespeare in the round
In Black Box Theater

The characters were full of heart
A false fog filled the air
The story was unfolded then
Of men and maidens fair

But summaries are not for me
Just go and see yourself
Is what I'd say were it still shown
But now it's on the shelf

So you should simply trust my word
And go to the next play
But not Jane Eyre because, bad news!
Its last show was today
