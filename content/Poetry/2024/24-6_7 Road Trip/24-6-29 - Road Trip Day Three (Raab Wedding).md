Mister and Missus Koen Rabb
Were married on this day
Their ceremony in a church
In the old fashioned way

Then once the couple had been wed
And out the doors processed
Family and friends soon followed them
To celebrate the blessed

Then heartfelt toasts and food and drink
Were had before the dance
Wonderful ways to end the day
Of solemnized romance

(Originally published as day four)
