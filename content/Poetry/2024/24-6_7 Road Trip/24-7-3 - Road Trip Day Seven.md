A card game and a mallet sport
Against fam'ly I play
Some winning moves I had today
In shanghai and croquet

Other than that, I had the joy
Of not riding a car
Cause we have reached our journey's end
We've traveled rather far

But now we rest in Montana
Where my grandparents live
That was the goal of our journey
To them, a visit give

And so we shall, for a few days
Of feasting and of fun
Spend lots of time together there
Before we have to run
