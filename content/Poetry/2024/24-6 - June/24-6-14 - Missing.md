While yesterday I lost my buds
Today I lost the bus
And standing here at the bus stop
I'd like to make a fuss

I don't ask much of my commute
Don't need reclining seat
It can be fast or very slow
In traffic stopped by tornado
Just put me on so I can go 
And see its route complete
