I see the people on the train
Going about their days
And in a moments glimpse of them
My mind is put to haze

Short seconds pass, I think they are
A distant well loved friend
But on a slightly longer glance
That thought comes to an end

Small world it is, but not that small
Except for when it is
Cause there has been a time or two
I've seen friends in the biz
