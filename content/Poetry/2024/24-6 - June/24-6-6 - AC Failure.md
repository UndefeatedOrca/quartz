Rattlin' rattlin' on the train
All 'cause AC's down again 
Rollin' rollin' down the tracks
Our cooling unit's getting lax
Spinnin' spinnin' go the wheels
"Not again!" Our laughter peals
Up and down the rails I roam 
Yet again I'll work from home
Drinkin' drinkin' all my water
Cause my desk is getting hotter
Apply now! Apply, you gotta 
Our office benefit's a sauna!
