Debate theory is just not fun
It's stale and old and lame
Conditionality debates
Are all about the same

One team reads blocks of bullet points
Too fast to really flow
And then they are rejoined with args 
Written decades ago

And while some points are straight up lies 
Like all args are condo
There are some more important points
That people ought to know

But most of those are washed away
While whining reigns supreme
And tacky args are painted as
Cause to reject the team

Those problems I can't fix alone
Repairs may take some time
But I can fix my own staleness
With metered verse and rhyme 

So I'm starting a new project
To fix poetic lack
And rewrite all my theory blocks
To be less prosiac 

So watch my instagram story
That's where I'll post my work
And if you have thoughts on my poems
Please comment, don't just lurk!
