It's time to go bed right now
I seem to need some sleep
After my work on counterplans
A win streak to upkeep

The JV div again
But if we keep on doing that
Where will we find fun then?

Perhaps the only thing to do
Is lock out open too
But that would be a bit harder
If I am to speak true

The team should be quite proud today
With wins across the board
In Novice, JV, Varsity
Be glad in what you've scored
