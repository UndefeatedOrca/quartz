The leaves are turning red from green
They're falling from their trees
And as the Autumn season comes
The nights begin to freeze
The chill sparks allergies and colds
So people start to sneeze
Mariah Carey starts to thaw
While hibernate do bees
But crisp Fall days remain things that
My weary heart will please
