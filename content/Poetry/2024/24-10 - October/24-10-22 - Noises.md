The noises sound above my head
At every time of day
The noises sound above my head
I don't know what to say

The ceiling rumbles constantly
I don't know what they do
The ceiling rumbles constantly
I know that much is true

The stampede on the next floor up
Continues in the night
The stampede on the next floor up
Sounds like a boxing fight

The noises high over my quad
Are like an anvil toss
The noises high over my quad
Might be heard from DeMoss

They're there at ten o'clock at night
They're there at six AM
They're there at ten o'clock at night
My mind frays like a hem

The guys who live a floor above
They make a constant noise
The guys who live a floor above
Us constantly annoy
