In peace I work in my dorm room
The door will block out sound
But blinds are open so I see
The beauty all around

And as I work on my homework
The setting sun I see
The reds and golds of lazy clouds
In fiery majesty

They wander slowly through the sky
Engulfed in radiant flame
The heavens in those twilit hours
Aren't ever made the same
