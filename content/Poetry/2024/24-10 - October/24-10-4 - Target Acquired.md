As I was walking up to East
A strange sight caught my eye
A girl had dropped her backpack and
On feet began to fly

For in her path another stood
But he began to run
And just as soon as it'd begun
The episode was done

Assassin had missed a target
The victim had survived
But huntress Kate stays on the prowl
So he may be short-lived
