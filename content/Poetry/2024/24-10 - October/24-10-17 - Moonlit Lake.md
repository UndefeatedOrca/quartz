A moment taken to relax
And just enjoy a view
Of moonlit lake and library
Is something good to do

The peaceful water burbles through
A fountain in the stone
While stars reflected in the lake
Can be pondered alone

Seated with none but God to hear
The thoughts that pass through me
I contemplate the beauty which
I am so blessed to see
