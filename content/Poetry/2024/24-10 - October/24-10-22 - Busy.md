I'm not sure how I manage it
But this Tuesday was full
Which can be awful frustrating
This day should be a lull

I don't have class once it's past noon
I should be free for hours
Instead I find myself engrossed
In nonsense that devours

So it will be tomorrow that
Will do work from today
'Cause I'm taking an early rest
A sleep debt to repay
