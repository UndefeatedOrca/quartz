I went to see the band today
I went to see them play
I went to see the band today
And they blew me away

I went to see a marching ba
And they made me feel old
I went to see the band today
And on their show I'm sold

I went to see the seniors play
Who once were my freshmen
They've kept the mello flame alive
And now they number ten!

I went to see my sister's band
And see her Red Queen's fate
That queen is also Drum Major
And she did really great

I went to see the band today
And by their work they've shown
That through the years that I've been gone
The band has truly grown
