Sometimes it's good to try new things
And do them just for fun
Like chasing frisbees on a play
At a complete dead run

Or gliding graceful on the floor
Of ballroom dancing club
Though graceful overstates my case
And overlooks my flubs

Or watching football past midnight
After a musical
Suffice to say I managed to
Keep my whole weekend full
