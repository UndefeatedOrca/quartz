The coming hordes of students rise
To take a taste of school
And as they look upon LU
They'll think it's pretty cool

But as for normal students, we
Will whine, and moan, and fuss
'Cause food lines will be a bit long
And kids will fill the bus

Remember though, before complaint,
That you did CFAW too
And ask yourself how you'd have felt
If students frowned at you
