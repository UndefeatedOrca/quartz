The restless traveler wanders through
With no one place to stay
He goes along a merry path
About his merry way

To stop and smell the flower's bloom
To wander in a rain
To clamber boulders on high hills
And stroll along the plain

The nomad marks a winding path
Without an end in mind
He wanders on forgotten paths
His final end to find
