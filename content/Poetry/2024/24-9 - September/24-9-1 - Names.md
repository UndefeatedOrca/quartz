The way to find out people's names
It turns out is to ask
And as our luck would have it
That's not a major task

'Cause people like to hear that word
Denoting who they are
Rememb'ring what that word could be
Will often take you far

While mem'ry isn't always great
It can be found improved
And when a name is remembered
People are greatly moved

So that's a thing I'm working on
I'm sorry if I fail
Someday I'll reach mem'ry's summit
For now I'm on the trail
