A tower shrouded in the fog
Illuminates the night
Its spectral visage lurks above
Leaking a pale blue light

Its outline dim and hard to see
In misty billows gray
Its azure light diffused throughout
Blue mockery of day

The obelisk to freedom stands
Silent in dim repose
Watching for signs of student life
As dreary fog still blows
