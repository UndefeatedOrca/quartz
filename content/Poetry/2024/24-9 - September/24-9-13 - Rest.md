A resting place is what I crave
My sanity to save

I have completed the first test
Now let me have my rest

The way for debates not to lose
Is maximize the snooze

For lack of sleep will bring me pain
And not provide a gain

So soon I hope I'll go sleep
My rest and joy to keep
