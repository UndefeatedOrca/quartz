The God who has painted the sky in its blue
And sunsets in oranges, reds, pinks, and rouges too
He fashioned the daisy and lilies each one
Providing them petals to wave in the sun
A tracing of delicate handiwork made
To show us His glory in beauty portrayed
To cause us to wonder in awe of the good
Inspire us to drink in as much as we could
Has also created the faces we see
On sidewalks, in chairs, even on the TV
With just the same craft, even more I suspect
No feature too small to forget or neglect
Intricate, beautiful, all are unique
With layers and depths in for those who would seek
He looks on us fondly like sunset above
But only we get to respond to His love
