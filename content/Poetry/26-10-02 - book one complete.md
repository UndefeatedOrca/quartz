Today I mostly read a book
My screen time is way down
But Frodo and the hobbit crew's
Beyond the barrow-downs

At current reading rate I ought
To maybe make it through
The trilogy in ample time
To other school do

But for the time, I can't complain
This textbook shall be done
And in the reading I shall have
An ample bit of fun
