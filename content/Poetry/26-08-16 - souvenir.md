A piece from last trip is sitting
Lodged there inside my hand
From loading tables in a truck
An injury unplanned

The wooden tables worked just fine
Except for simply this
A shard of them is still with me
Less than a man would miss

It's sat there pleasant for a week
But even so I hope
That someday soon my splinter shall
Decide to go elope
