Now that all's done
Wrapped up from the fun
My laundry's still there
Though it's not unfair
It isn't the best
That rather than rest
The washer will go
While the dryer is slow
Because when off the road
Then it's time for a load
To be gathered again
Out of all that's once been
Washed in last week
For in vain quest we seek
To have clothes that stay clean
But what hope's there is mean
For a thing such as that
Will not come to old Pat
