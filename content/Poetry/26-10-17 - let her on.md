Please get my sister on a plane
She's been there the whole day
Just waiting in the airport lounge
Without nowhere else to stay

She's juggled somehow (beanbags whence)
She played cornhole alone
The LEGO store had things in stock
Read book, looked at her phone

Her handstands are improved a bit
Her playlists getting stale
She's walked the terminals four times
Her sanity will fail

So get my sister on a plane
Please
