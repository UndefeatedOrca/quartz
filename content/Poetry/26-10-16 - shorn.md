My head is lighter than it was
My shoulders freer now
A weight has lifted off of me
And fallen off the bow

My worries still remain with me
The troubles of the world
And even now the scroll of fear
Has followed me unfurled

The drowning doom of doubt still looms
The wreath of woe still stands
But even so there's still more lift
And so I feel quite grand
