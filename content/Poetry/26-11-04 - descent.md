An envelope of darkness closed
Below a block of cloud
Above a black night sky has stayed
The light of stars, all cowed

Just wing and capsule in the vast
Expanse of nothing-air
No birds, no breath, just engine's thrum
A place of deep nowhere

Descending now, the signal lights
Dip in to lower mist
To fill the gloom with burst of light
Where gentle wing has kissed

Awash without a gloom to see
Now red and white will flash
Like sirens of forgotten doom
Like silent cymbal's crash

Down and down, fog canyons wind
Some deep, some narrow path
And still the lights are burning bright
Descending with cool wrath

But finally through quakes and bumps
The lower clouds do break
Domestic lights and warehouses
Now viewing eyes can make

No doom was brought by blackness full
Nor did the fog sustain
But even still great joy was found
When fin'lly on the plain
