I wish that I could patch up all
The schisms that I see
Avert the crash of train which will
Be coming close to me

Defend my friends from every harm
Before they know its there
I wish I could, I wish I might
But life just isn't fair

So put your house in order please
And reconcile life
For there is only so far down
I'll meddle in some strife
