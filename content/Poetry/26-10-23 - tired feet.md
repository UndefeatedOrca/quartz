Freedom Uncensored was guarding a door
Which ought to have locked but obstruction deplored
My feet got quite antsy while standing out there
So when sessions had started it only seemed fair
To frolic and dance in a Grand Lobby waltz
Which filled me with joy, despite all foot faults
But work was not over, or so I had thought
Only corrected when returned I had got
So I went to the sojourners gathered with food
And met some good people who often elude
Which turned into helping them clean up a mess
Created in part by my things, I confess
American flag, rubber duck, and a book
Possessing more documents than thought at first look
But greater in volume were all of the globes
Though where they'd all come from ain't in my brain lobes
Eventually all of that was all packed
And my feet which had all of this time gone and tracked
Were finally given a respite for now
Until under weight of tomorrow they bow
