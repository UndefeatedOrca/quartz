A friendship's cornerstone is trust
That's built in fits and starts
By memories that you both share
And opening of hearts

Developing integrity
You hold their private thoughts
Withholding truth, and telling lies
Are how unity rots

While sharing of the inmost soul
Creates a deeper link
As bond of fellowship are formed
Alike begin to think

You face the world, not alone
Together we can fight
Together we can face the day
And maybe find the light
