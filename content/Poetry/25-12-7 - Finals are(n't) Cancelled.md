The finals have begun in style
With cancelling them all
Until professors think about
The consequence of call

And now the snow day's awful weird
As people do their test
Despite the closure of the school
They'll have to do their best

So things are getting kind of weird
But just hold on my friend
A few more days and we will come
To make it to the end
