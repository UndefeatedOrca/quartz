The swing dancing is starting soon
Once little feet have fled
Then music will begin to play
As some of you are led

The swing dancing is starting soon
At conversation end
The jig will stream into the room
And jolly feet will wend

The swing dancing is starting soon
But first we'll have a nap
A joyful noise will ring aloud
Hear happy shoes a'tap

The swing dancing is starting soon
Or maybe will next week
And the the cheerful sound will laugh
To hear the floorboards creak
