I think that maybe Saturday
Disciples stayed up late
Discussing what to about
Their Teacher's grisly fate

And so while liveliest of all
Would wake up with the dawn
The others would then take a while
To get up with a yawn

They didn't know just what they'd see
Except for tomb of rock
