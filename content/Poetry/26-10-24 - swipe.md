Borrowing food from a friend of mine's plate
Acquiring all to my appetite sate
Providing a providence passing the hope
That plates will be polished before they elope
To caterers who in collecting our haul
Will not be so burdened by some or by all
But rather their rhythms will rest in delight
As I take the tenderloin for one more bite
