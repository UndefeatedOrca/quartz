Wandering the darkened streets
While searching for our food
We start to find frustration loom
Into our shadowed mood

The pub was too upscale for us
Italian restaurant too
The next place was an hour wait
Which we refused to do

But finally in pondering
And wandering in plight
A reservation was procured
To everyone's delight
