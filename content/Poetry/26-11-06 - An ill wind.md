The wind goes over the mountaintops
Disturbing leaves from the trees
And tells the tales from a far off land
In the guise of a whispering breeze

The wind goes down to the valley floor
Along with a torrent of rain
To tidings bring of a distant war
And the dread and the dark and the pain

The wind goes winding to find a house
Where hoping a woman still waits
To see the man who she once had loved
And to see him unmastered by fate

The wind is chill in the dread and dark
From journeys it's made from afar
Where nary a joy is awake in the night
And the watchman sees nary a star

The wind has watched a battalion fall
Dark arrows descend in the night
A battle unopened concluded ere start
No survivor is left from the fight

The wind is racing the cavalry
And close on its heels they descend
Now doom that was felt in an icy touch
Is seen as the black blades now rend

The wind is over a lonely world
As darkness gives way to the dawn
What hope can be raised in the brightness of day
When the heroes are all dead and gone
