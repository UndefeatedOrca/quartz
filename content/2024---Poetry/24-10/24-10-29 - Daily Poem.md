I'm not sure why the people like
To read my daily poem
Producing them is not that hard
You could make them at home

But I appreciate your views
And hope you like the post
But as for skill in poetry
I have nothing to boast

An iamb here, a rhyme or two
The ballad form employed
And that's about all that I do
I'm glad that they're enjoyed
