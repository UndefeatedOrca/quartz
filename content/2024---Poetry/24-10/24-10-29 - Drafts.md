While digging through my drafts I found
A few amazing lines
That took my nice opinions and
Really gave them a shine

But they remain in private now
'Cause I can't find the time
To polish up that olden prose
For now I choose to rhyme

So dust and cobwebs they acquire
Though tarnish they do not
So someday I may just return
To finish up those thoughts
