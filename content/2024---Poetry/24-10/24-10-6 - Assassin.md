I had a dream wherein I stood
In line outside the rot
I turned around, Landon was there
And then I had been got

But in the dream I was okay
With chronos on my side
The safe time after convo would
The game keep me inside

Assassin has consumed my mind
While waking and asleep
I stay alert and watch my back
Survival to upkeep
