Another round for us to fight
Another tourney won
So with the end of this brief stint
Our JV time is done

They're putting us in open now
We broke our leg in quarts
So now we go to where they put
The excellent in sports

I don't expect to win a lot
At least not at the start
But what we lack in speed and skill
We'll make up with more heart
