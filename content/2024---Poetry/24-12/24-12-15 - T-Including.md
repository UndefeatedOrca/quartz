I'm not sure how they find these things
Defining up the words
But some of these assertions made
Are simply just absurd

The meanings are inverted and
You won't tell anyone
That what you've said does not apply
For that would be no fun

But as for me I'll take your work
And give it a new shine
To bolster up our topic files
For all your cards are mine
