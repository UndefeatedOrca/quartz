We sing our songs of praise each week
We sing them without fail
For holy truths are such a gift
That earthly suff'rings pale

So leap from hope that's in your heart
And cherish all your peace
For in your heart there burns a thought
Which won't let praises cease

The greatest gift that seems too good
Is truly not a ploy
It lights the fires of our hearts
And fills to brim with joy
