The snow goes tumbling through the air
The ground is painted white
The flakes are all aglow in air
They make their final flight

Descending from the sky above
They dance along a breeze
And blasts of wind decide their path
To bring them where they please

Chicago has brought joy and fun
I'm glad I chose to go
We'll finish strong, one last hurrah
For we've been gifted snow!
