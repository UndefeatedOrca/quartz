Across the town we drove along
To spread a bit of joy
To sing some rousing Christmas songs
And our coaches annoy

With Buble, Carey, and ole Wham!
Plus Carol of the Bells
In multi-part and harmony
It somehow kind of gels

Our journey lasted through the night
With songs on radio
And it was cold enough to freeze
Chill to frostbite our toes
But overall I think we're glad
That we did choose to go
