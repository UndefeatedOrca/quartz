Here is a blink or miss it poem
That is not very deep
And that's because I'm writing this
So I can get to sleep

I stayed up late while playing Civ
And now it's past midnight
Someday perhaps I'll check the clock
Before I'm in such plight

But for today I'll simply say
That I must get to bed
Or else I'll fall asleep down here
On keyboard lay my head
