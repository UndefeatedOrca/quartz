A secret writ where it's unseen
Kept in its hiding place
Cannot always be hid the best
When it's in cyberspace

Faceless is one behind a screen
But he still has a name
And once a delver finds his post
Things can't be quite the same

So wisely plumb the depths online
Or you'll be forced to care
For what is seen can't be unseen
Whether or not it's fair
