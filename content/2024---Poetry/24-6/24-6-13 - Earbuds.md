Overflowed my pockets did
As I boarded the bus
But soon they would be emptier
Which made me want to cuss

Cause in my pockets I possessed
Earbuds, wallet, and phone
That is, until I left the bus
Leaving my 'buds alone

My pockets' size was miniscule
My stuff was far to large
That stuff fell out was no surprise
That's what my buds did, I surmise
And had I been to that fact wise
They'd still be mine to charge

So now I go to Amazon 
To order a new pair
It's sad but true the saying that
Life just isn't fair
