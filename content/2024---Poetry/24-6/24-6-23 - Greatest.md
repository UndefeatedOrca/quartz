The greatest man who ever lived
Was born of a virgin
He cast out demons into swine
With good and evil He did dine
He told branches He was the vine 
And came to conquer sin

The greatest man who ever lived
His end was full of strife 
The crown of thorns upon His head
In unfair trial His charges read
Put on a cross to make Him dead
But He was raised to life 

The greatest man who ever lived
Is still alive today
And through His perfect sacrifice
Where nails through holy hands did slice
I'll see His face in paradise
For He my debt did pay
