Ten years ago my sis was born
That's an entire decade
And through her whole action filled life
Great joy for us she's made

So while I will argue with her
And sometimes we might fight
I won't deny that to our lives
She brings a lot of light

So Sundreana have some fun
And celebrate this day
'Cause there is only just one time
That is your tenth birthday
