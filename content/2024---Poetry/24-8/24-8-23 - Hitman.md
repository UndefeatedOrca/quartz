The secret to an epic game
Is finding small rule flaws
Exploiting them to win the game
To thunderous applause

But even when the ruleset's clear
And somebody has won
Sometimes the losers say they win
Just 'cause the rules aren't fun

When they say that you look at them
But simply let them be
The guys who know the rules can see
Who took the victory
