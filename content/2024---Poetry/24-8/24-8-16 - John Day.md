The first true day of freedom done
A restful day at last
'Tween pickleball and super smash
A good full day has past

I started reading a new book
And met with my mentees
And ate at least three meals at Slims
While rain fell through the trees

I'm not sure what else I should say
'Cause it has gotten late
And when I've stayed up for this long
My thoughts aren't always great
