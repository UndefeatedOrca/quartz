The year's road trip comes to a close
At least it does for me
'Cause I wanted to go to a
Conf'rence and left early

So I've gotten home on a plane
With some minor delays
It could be worse, I've missed no flights
So what else can I say

It's almost time to get work
And return to that grind
But if you check out CRA
No trace of me you'll find

So if you've missed my smiling face
Greatly while I've been gone
Perhaps you'll see that face again
If you attend NatCon
