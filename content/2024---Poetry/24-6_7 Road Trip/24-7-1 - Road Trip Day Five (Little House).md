Once Laura Ingalls Wilder lived 
Upon the Plum Creek banks 
And for her stories of that time
Her readers give their thanks

And in that town of Walnut Grove
There are some galleries
That talk about the little house
Books and TV series

The union of the two is odd
Frontier and fiction mix
While artifacts and movie props
The strange mood do not fix

While it's an interesting place
I wouldn't go again
Unless I fully read read the books
I'd think about it then

A little further down the road
Is a fam'ly homestead
Where Mrs Ingalls Wilder lived
Just visit it instead
