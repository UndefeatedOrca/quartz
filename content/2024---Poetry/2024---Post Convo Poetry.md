## 17:
It's the very last convo of this year's grand streak
And all of you've loved the poems each week
So it's time to remember the age-old tradition
And gather soon rotside after convocation

And finding a way to end the year well
Dondi will work a rhetorical spell
Wending together nouns and adverbs
His speech and the worship'll pass by in two blurs

But after that time which seemeth so short
Only one action with good sense doth comport
And that is to journey out to the Southwest
Joining the lunch friends that you know best

LU Meet can't be beat for a post-convo meal
And if you've got a mealplan the price is a steal
You've been there before on many occasion
But in case you've forgotten for some complex reason

Where we go feasting is tested and true
You know it for cookies, ice cream, and cold brew
It stands gleaming brightly right next to the shaft
(the Commons Four name at which everyone laughed)

That's right, you guessed it, the place is the rot
Once you have entered and food you have got
Then travel downstairs to find us if you please
The place where we sit you'll locate with ease

Head East from the stairs and move on your way
For you'll find us sitting near the ole' PDA
Maybe Braedon will be there with his yellow phone
And at most of his puns you'll do nothing but groan

Or perhaps Jarren will speak on his favorite subjects
Like his incredible hatred of all architects
And Josh may just be there with his many rings

And of doofus Johnathan be not uncouth

And speaking of me, your poet and friend
I must depart for the poem's at an end
But fear not, for of me you won't be lacking
You'll see me at post-convo rotting!
