My Carolina trip is here
For this year's last debate
And here at Wake we'll take our stand
We'll weather love and hate

With a new partner and new args
We'll see what we can do
And if the the thing goes off the rails
Well we can always sue

Prepare the trumpets and trombones
Prepare the vic'try bell
Prepare to gird your loins for war
For fears are what we quell

These battles will be fought with words
And we will fight our best
The truest way to find your strength
Is put it to the test
