I won't exactly say for sure
The reason I have writ
A couple extra poems today
That have taken a bit

But what I'll say is that I'm not
Always the best talker
Especially when I chose to
Go up and talk to her

So I'll leave all my drafts alone
My eyes only shall see
And what shall come in future days
Well what will be will be
