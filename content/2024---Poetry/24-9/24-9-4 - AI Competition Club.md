The bots won't take away the world
If I have things to say
But when it comes to AI tools
No shame in use or play

It will assert a shift in life
If you like it or not
So why not give the stuff a chance
And not dismiss the thought

These were my thoughts as I went to
The AI club today
Twas a good choice that I have made
I think I'm there to stay
