A massive box with a TV
Has appeared in my dorm
With this new tech I think we will
Make a return to form 

Boot up the ole Nintendo switch
And then we can play smash
Where characters beloved from games
Engage in mortal clash

The picture in this thing is good
Far better than the last
But that's prob'bly because it's not
A screen from decades past
