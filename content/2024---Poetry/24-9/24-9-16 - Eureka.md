The greatest minds will think alike
And come the same thought
But just 'cause two are of one mind
The smartest makes them not

A lightbulb moment shared by two
Can drive things more complex
And when a smart guy thinks like you
Well that can be a flex

Collaborate to innovate
And think while standing tall
For something might come if you throw
A restaurant at a wall

[Specifically an Italian restaurant]
