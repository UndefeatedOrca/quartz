Lord God, creator of the heavens and the earth and ruler over the wind and the rains. In your infinite wisdom and power, we humbly ask that you release upon us a white blanket of snow, that we would have the opportunity both to find joy in its fluffy goodness and be freed of the stress of school for a morning or day; if it be your will, please grant us the weather we seek. Amen.
