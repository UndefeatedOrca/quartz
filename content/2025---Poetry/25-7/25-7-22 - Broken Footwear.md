I used to have a working shoe 
Before it fell apart 
And tore the sole away from me
And with it my poor heart

Sure it was tearing, lost its shine
And looked a little scuffed
But fixing that was doable 
If it had been rebuffed 

But now I think my shoe is toast
It can't be fixed, I know
And when I step it flaps around
I wish it wasn't so
