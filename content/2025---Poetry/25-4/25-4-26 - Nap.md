I went about my day today
And had a lot of fun
Just look at what comes after this
And I'll give the full run

But this thing will focus on
Was an essential part
A thing that I have not done much
That is a old lost art

I took a nap in midst of day
It was a real nice time
I got take a few minutes
Of rest that was sublime

And though the sheep would flee my count
I fin'lly numbered them
And so I got a little sleep
If not an total REM
