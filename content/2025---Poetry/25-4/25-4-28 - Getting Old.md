I think I need to stop this bit
But it is far too fun
To drop into the broader world
When there might be the one

I've used it more than once this year
To an unknown effect
I hope that it's been too subtle
For others to detect

But I suppose I must go on 
And surreptitiously
Continue in this noble quest
And go and write some more
