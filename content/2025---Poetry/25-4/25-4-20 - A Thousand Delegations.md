A thousand delegations
From every tribe and tongue
A thousand generations
Who've all the same hymn sung

A thousand saints and martyrs
Who hold the ancient creed 
A thousand kings cast down their crowns 
And all the Good Book read

A thousand branches grafted
To Israel's holy tree 
A thousand congregations
One church in unity 

Two thousand years that body's stood 
To just begin its tale 
Ten thousand more it sure will stand
For hell will not prevail

And thousands strong the holy men
Before the throne will kneel
So let not class or race divide
And share the holy meal

Originally composed 12/24/22 for the Liberty Unity in Verse contest (where it did not make it out of the qualifying round)
Published for Easter in 2025
