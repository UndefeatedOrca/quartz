The hidden skills my friends all have
Surprise me to this day
And Super Smashing competence
Is one of those I'd say

So while she's novice at the game
A future is in reach
To make a new career for her
In play of Princess Peach

---
The subject of this poem was Alice, who played well for her first time playing Smash
