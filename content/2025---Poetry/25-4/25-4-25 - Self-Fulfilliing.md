I hate that I can write a thing
With innocent intent
And people read into the text
Some words that I've not sent

But then a couple days later
The thing they say comes true
I think that this is awfuler
Than falling ill with flu

But then again the bug is there
Without an easy fix
And if you're reading, comprehend
Please further thoughts all nix
