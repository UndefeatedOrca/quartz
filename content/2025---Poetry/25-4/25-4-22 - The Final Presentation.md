There is no doubt we planned it out
As best as we could do
We wrote the paper yesterday
The presentation too

It was a decent bit of time
We took the whole evening
And by the end of what we'd done
We had a finished thing

And in the morning, once I'd woke
I made is to the class
And got to see if what I'd made
Could then the real test pass

Who needs to practice when you've got
A decent set of slides
It's not as if on this good pitch
My grade for this course rides

And after all it turned out well
If just a little tense
We managed to turn what had
Into a piece of sense

The moral of the story is
To just send it, that's all
And if that don't work out for you
Please don't, my number, call
