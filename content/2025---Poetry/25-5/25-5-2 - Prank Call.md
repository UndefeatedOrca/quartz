While sitting in the library
I start to overhear
A thing that sent me off the end
In ludicrousy sheer

A man had made a small prank call
He voiced like Donald Duck
But with that strong impression he
Found that he had some luck

The girl out on the other end
Three thousand miles away
Would go with what the prankster said
The game along she played

Eventually he got to jokes
Beyond the Bee Movie
He wondered if there was a chance
That she he'd someday see

I don't know what he did for it
But hit it off they had
I hope that they are happy though
It made me a bit sad
