We've traveled far to get back home
We filled the car with stuff
I've put my clothes in the washer
Unpacked more than enough

The only thing that's left to do
Is settle in back home
Away from pulse and tide of school
And once more old haunts roam

So we will see what summer brings
We'll see what I can learn
And maybe if I'm lucky too
I'll see what I can earn
