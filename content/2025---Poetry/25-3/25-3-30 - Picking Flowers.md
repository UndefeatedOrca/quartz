We clambered down the wooded hill
To gather up from stems
The golden daffodils down there
Which shine like precious gems

This morn along the wooded road
In place I cannot say
We gathered up the flowers to
Create a fresh bouquet

The brilliant yellow blooms would
In water bottle vase
I hope that they have gone to make
Another smiling face
