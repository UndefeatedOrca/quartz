"I think the storm will come later
It won't be awfully soon
You've got some time before you're hit
With tropical monsoon"

At least that's what my friend told me
As we wandered outside
But I did not have so much faith
I thought her phone had lied

For falling from the cloudy sky
Was water cold and wet
But while precipitated on 
She still predicts "not yet"

---
If I recall correctly, this was written in reference to Kate thinking that the raijn wouldn't start yet (when the rain had already started)
