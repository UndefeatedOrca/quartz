The weather's great and sun is out
Though chilly in the morn
I can't deny the joy of Spring
Where anew world is born

The flowers bloom and trees take leaf
Again the warmth is out
I can't deny the joy of Spring
And thus must give a shout

The days grow longer, longer still
Expose me to the sun
I can't deny the joy of Spring
And won't decry the fun
