The rot has food that can be good
I've really got to say
If you go to 360 Grill
Specifically Tuesday

I think Thursdays are tasty too
With quesadilla runs
And rot dogs always are rolling
To put inside a bun

The burgers from fresh off the grill
And waffles that you make
Or salad from the salad bar
Is something that I'll take

I'd be remiss to not mention
On Monday's Bistro's got
A flatbread pizza you can make
And that they'll bake real hot

The ice cream can be tasty too
And though I've not seen them
The double chocolate cookies are
A truly precious gem

So though the experts disagree
I must commend the space
That can provide for every meal
While I live in this place
