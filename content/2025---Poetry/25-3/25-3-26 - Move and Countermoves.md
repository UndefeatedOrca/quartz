I dance the slowly tilting plane
As round the world spins
I search for answers, all I get
Are many more questions

What stirs the hearts of humans here
And drives them in their acts
It's more complex than I would think
I need more than just facts

And though I love advice givers
They can be quite naive
So while their input 's kindly made
There're things they don't perceive

But as the rolling earth will go
Regardless of my fate
There's nothing more to do in life
Except appreciate
