The time to go to bed is past
The time to wake is soon
A dozen hours lie between
The time that's now and noon

The minutes flow into the hours
And hours flow in to days
But all I'm left with is lost time
As I move through the haze

Somehow three days of school are past
Before I could but blink
It doesn't quite make sense to me
No matter how I think
