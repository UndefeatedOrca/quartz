The open sky and clouds and light
Are what the trees reach toward
They give me hope when all is lost
They show a path forward

Just like a plant desires food
So I desire sun
To feed my soul and harness life
To give me fuel for fun

To photosynthesize a bit
Just for the old times' sake
A moment in the sun or two
Is something I will take
