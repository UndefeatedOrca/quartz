A mystery I can't quite crack
Enigma wrapped queries
Which I will leave for others to
Explain with a new theories

A faceless nameless mask would hold
My interest more or less
But limits to my knowledge still
Concern me I confess

So what to do I ponder on
'Cause patience can't be beat
While I still seek to find out who's
That unknown factor
