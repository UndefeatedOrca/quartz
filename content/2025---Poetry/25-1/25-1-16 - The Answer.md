The broken people walking by
With eyes downcast
In blue light cast
Will try to find out why

Why are we on the blasted earth?
Why do men die?
Why should you try?
Where is the joy? Where is the mirth?

From far beyond the realm of space: 
"I give you sight
I AM the Light"
Booms loudest voice from highest place

The Lord of lords and King of kings
The Way, the Truth
And old and youth
To God on high all sing

"We praise you for your every deed
We love you Lord
Our eyes are toward
Wherever you shall lead"
