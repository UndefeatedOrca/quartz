Debate papers have been strewn all over the internet and hosted everywhere and nowhere at once. While some journals are still hosted on academic sites, the death of debate scholarship is evident in the impossibility of finding Contemporary Argumentation and Debate. You'll find issue 38 on the JMU website, but other than that, most issues have been lost to time and the Wayback Machine. I've attempted to centrally locate all of the pieces here for your reading pleasure.
