Did you know that Domino’s has a [wedding registry](https://www.dominosweddingregistry.com/)? I didn’t either, but now you know.

Not sure why this exists, but I guess it makes money?

Similarly, did you know about the Vegas Taco Bell [wedding package](https://www.tacobellwedding.com/products/wedding-package)? They don’t provide a marriage license, but they do have a chapel, sauce packet garter and bowtie, T-shirts, a cinnabon cake, and tacos, so if you ever find yourself a spouse interested in getting married and/or renewing their vows in a Taco Bell, look into it (I guess).
