I used this [Your Name in Landsat - NASA Science](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/landsat/outreach/your-name-in-landsat/) website to send a bunch of my friends their name in Landsat pictures. It was a ton of fun. I highly recommend the exercise.

Reactions included: “what,” “is this the NASA thing?,” “how did you do this,” and sending me back my own name. All in all, a great success.
