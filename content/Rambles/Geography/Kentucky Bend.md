Kentucky Bend, KY, or Bubbleland[^1] is a 26.9 square mile area completely surrounded by Tennessee and Missouri.

![Map of Kentucky Bend with states shaded in different colors](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!E1zP!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fac41916e-1a90-40b3-b983-b1e61cf6e3f0_720x720.png)

What?

Yes, that’s right, when they were determining the state borders, they weren’t clear on where the Mississippi flowed, so somehow it was made part of Kentucky.

Or I should say Virginia. The border is so old that when it was established, there wasn’t such a thing as Kentucky, or Tennessee for that matter, it was still part of North Carolina.

An uncited claim on Wikipedia also says that Tennessee claimed the land until 1848, and I choose to believe it.

While over 300 people lived in The Bend in 1880, there are now only 9 residents.

_The more you know._
