You may not know this, but the Taft Memorial in Washington D.C. has a carillon on it which is played annually by the Carillonneur of the Capitol. You can read more about that here: [Meet the Carillonneur of the Capitol | Architect of the Capitol](https://www.aoc.gov/explore-capitol-campus/blog/meet-carillonneur-capitol)
