## What if there's a second Harold?
Aside from the visual similarity, there is not a second Harold. This article shows us why: [Liberty University remembers longtime supporter George Rawlings](https://wset.com/news/local/liberty-university-remembers-longtime-supporter-george-rawlings-lynchburg-virginia-march-2023)

>==George Rawlings==, a Kentucky businessman, and entrepreneur passed away on March 16 at age 77. He is survived by his wife of 56 years, Beverly Sue Rawlings; his ==three brothers, Herbert, Harold, and Carrol==; and many nieces and nephews.

There are four Rawlings brothers, there are no repeated names.

>The family’s ties to Liberty go back to the university’s early years. ==Their father, the late Dr. John Rawlings==, was a longtime pastor who served as a key advisor to Liberty founder Dr. Jerry Falwell in the establishment of the university.
>
>The John W. Rawlings School of Divinity, housed in the Freedom Tower, is named in his honor. The Rawlings family helped plan many unique features in the building, including the scriptorium, which displays rare books and Bibles valued at over $1 million from the Rawlings’ personal collection. John Rawlings also served on Liberty’s Board of Trustees; ==Harold Rawlings is a current board member.==

>Liberty Campus Pastor Jonathan Falwell said ==his father and Herb Rawlings== were roommates at ==Baptist Bible College== in Springfield, Mo., in the early 1950s and that’s where the relationship with the family began.

This isn't definitive, but recall that Harold also had a degree from Baptist Bible College.[^2]
