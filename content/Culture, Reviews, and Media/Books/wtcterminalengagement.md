I finished Wearing the Cape: Terminal Engagement by Marion G. Harmon today and boy was it a fun one. The series returns to form with Astra doing her superhero thing and power-scaling continuing, though in a measured way. I think a few of the choices may be a bit risky, but to discuss them I would need to spoil the book and

1. None of you except my mother have read even the first book (Wearing the Cape), but I think all of you should, and

2. One of you might have read the first book, but whether you have or not, you still have my copy of it, and I would recommend you read it and maybe give it back to me when you’re done. I have the following books if you want to borrow them as well.

For those of you who don’t own physical copies, I’m pretty sure that the series is free with Kindle Unlimited, and you can also buy a copy here: [Wearing the Cape on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B004XRCC1G?ref_=dbs_m_mng_rwt_calw_tkin_0&storeType=ebooks). It’s also possible that the author will make the first few kindle books free around Christmas, but honestly, don’t wait.[^1]

The audiobooks are great too.

[^1]: I have no comments at all on whether/if this series contains a major fictional crush, but if you read the series, feel free to send your guesses to me.
