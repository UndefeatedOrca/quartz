[Minesweeper Rush](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3665840/MineSweeper_Rush/), originally published as Minesweeper Tetris before the creator received a letter informing them that the name was in violation of Tetris copyright, is a free steam game that features an infinitely generating minesweeper board which clear a row each time either all mines or all safe spaces are identified. If the board reaches the top of the screen, your game is over (the Tetris element).

I am pretty good.

When Minesweeper Rush released, the leaderboards from Minesweeper Tetris didn't get ported over, and so the field was open for an enterprising player to snag top spots on the leaderboard. I did so.

On May 13th 2025, I claimed a short-lived top spot in the Hard game mode category.
