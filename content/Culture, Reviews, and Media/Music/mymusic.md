This is a list of all the ensembles I've played in

- Elementary School Band - bad, do not discuss, I'm not convinced I actually played during concerts

- Middle School Band - bad, do not discuss, I definitely played a few notes during concerts

- [High School Concert Band](https://www.robinsonband.org/) - This is when I finally began to practice in earnest (learning the incredible lesson that when you practice things you get better at them). By senior year, I was in the top band. Some fun pieces we played include [Fantasy on American Sailing Songs](https://www.windrep.org/Fantasy_on_American_Sailing_Songs), [Emperata Overture](https://www.windrep.org/Emperata_Overture), [Homage to Bharat](https://www.windrep.org/Homage_to_Bharat), [Give Us This Day](https://www.windrep.org/Give_Us_This_Day), and [March of the Resistance](https://www.windrep.org/March_of_the_Resistance).

- Marching Band - The pinnacle of my high school experience, we played three shows during my time with the band: [Everybody Knows](https://youtu.be/fD9qDM4KF6Y?si=7SdvJxzgn6kqYJ76), [Dark Horse](https://youtu.be/s8sZ_Wv2efw?si=UWMkIvdv653PlouS), and [Winter Sketches](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1-iHjeRaI3UC98tg6qq2FLLKnrg6F7Gzj/view?usp=sharing). I had the honor of serving as Mellophone Section Leader

- [Main Street Community Band](https://www.fairfaxband.org/main-street-community-band/) ([Youtube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCgiRdFpt-9BRZr3vJgBufKw)) - I joined this community band while in community college and played several concerts with them. It's a lovely group, and if you're in the Fairfax area, I highly recommend joining up. Some of my favorite pieces included [Affirmation](https://youtu.be/DYaVMbVAZwU?si=B5PLfVnsBzCWGbaX), [Casey at the Bat](https://youtu.be/5eRUBvtO2Io?si=xknJtPsQquiH1mU5), [Declaration Overture](https://www.windrep.org/Declaration_Overture), and [Holiday Piece](https://youtu.be/q0aixbXGt5Q?si=RFwmOHhVlx20wOL_).

- Liberty University Band - This non-auditioned wind band at Liberty has been my musical home for the past 3 years, though unfortunately I won't be returning for the 2026 spring semester due to a scheduling conflict. We've played some fun pieces including [Scootin' on Hard Rock](https://www.windrep.org/Scootin%E2%80%99_on_Hardrock), [Jungle Fantasy](https://www.windrep.org/Jungle_Fantasy), and [Rocketship](https://www.windrep.org/Rocketship!)
