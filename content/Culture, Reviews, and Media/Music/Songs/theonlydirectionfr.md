I've been going through my downloads folder and glanced upon several fascinating subjects, most of which I won't discuss.

What I will discuss is the file that I found titled: [The Only Direction I Know For Real](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fII8rPAivpM) (I somehow managed to lose the file as I was writing this, so that was interesting).

It is a mashup of The Only Thing I Know For Real and What Makes You Beautiful. I could listen to it for hours. The way that the melodies overlap, mesh together, meld, it's incredible. It's a nutritious musical meal packaged with a really good song and a song that I am unwilling to assess critically for fear of my sanity.

Go listen to it, you won't regret it (much).
