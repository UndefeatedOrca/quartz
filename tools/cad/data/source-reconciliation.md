# Source reconciliation — 2026-09-15

## What is established

- The original 220 PDFs remain unchanged. New files retain their original publisher/EBSCO bytes except two documented extracts from the existing volume-38 full issue.
- All 141 distinct EBSCO Communication & Mass Media Complete records in the saved journal search now have a local PDF match. Metadata exports were not treated as full-text downloads. Source IDs and stable detail links are in `ebsco-records.json` and the manifest.
- Volumes 22–23, 25–30, 34–35 and 37 were actually published: their acquired PDFs and bibliographic records establish this. No volume number is classified as unpublished.
- Volume 24: the pre-existing forum compilation has only its cover and pages 16–101. Its cover lists the featured article, ten forum pieces and two reviews. These now have individual PDFs; EBSCO additionally identifies the review on pages 114–118, verified in that PDF. Printed pages 1–118 are covered. The original compilation remains available.
- Volume 38: [JMU's contents](https://commons.lib.jmu.edu/cad/) and the entire 196-page local issue were reconciled. All listed articles, preface, introduction and afterword are present. The editor's welcome, journal information, contents and contributor biographies were also inventoried. The latter three link into the full issue. Printed pages 1–191 correspond to one-based PDF pages 6–196. Welcome: printed iii / PDF 3. Introduction: printed 3–10 / PDF 8–15. Extraction boundaries and parent checksum are stored in the manifest.
- Volume 38's welcome explicitly describes a six-year publication hiatus following the 2017 volume. This establishes a hiatus, not missing volume numbers or inferred annual issues.
- Volume 36's five EBSCO records are matched and present. The PDF establishes the title “Making Debate Normal: The Texas Normal Debating League's Democratic and Pedogogical Functions”; the printed spelling is retained.

## Bibliographic corrections

Opening pages and closing review signatures were checked when metadata disagreed. Evidence and original metadata are preserved in the manifest, and the unmodified EBSCO bibliography remains separately available.

- Volume 22 contains image scans. Titles, bylines and starting printed pages were visually reviewed before import. The two generic “Book Reviews” records are reviews of *Smart Tournament Administrator* (Konrad W. Hack) and *Tab Room on the PC* (Jon Bruschke). Adjacent review fragments on shared printed pages remain in the original source PDFs.
- Volume 23's value-debate forum is a collection spanning pages 72–100, with Brian R. McGee as editor and contributions by Michael Bartanen, David M. Berube, Dale A. Herbeck, John P. Katsulas and Linda M. Collier. The entire collection is retained.
- Volume 24's three reviewers are Gordon Stables, Christopher Skiles and Mark R. Crossman, not the review editor T. C. Winebrenner. “Sndier” was corrected to Alfred C. Snider from the PDF byline.
- Complete author lists for the volume-27 digital futures article and volume-34 presidential war powers article were recovered from their opening pages.
- Volume 34's PDF identifies 2013/2014; volume 35's identifies 2015/2016 while EBSCO indexes it as 2016. Dates were not calculated from volume numbers.

## Remaining gaps and limits

The following two volume-2 PDFs have not been located:

1. Raymond Zeuschner and Charlene Hill, *Psychological presumption: its place in value topic debate*. A [contemporary paper held by ERIC](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED207119.pdf) cites the piece in *Contributions on the Philosophy and Practice of CEDA* (1981), including a reference to page 21. That paper is not the missing article and was not substituted for it.
2. Jack Howe, *CEDA's objectives: lest we forget*. [NEDA's objectives](https://nedadebate.com/about/neda-objectives-procedures/) and [UVM's debate bibliography](https://www.uvm.edu/~debate/debatebib.html) corroborate the publication. The bibliography gives *CEDA Yearbook* 2 (1981), pages 1–3. No matching PDF was located.

Earlier CEDA Yearbook material already in volumes 1–16 remains included. Independent full tables of contents for volumes 1–23 and 25–37 could not be recovered during this run. The former [CEDA journal site](http://www.cedadebate.org/CAD/index.php/CAD) and attempted Wayback archive pages were unavailable through the research tools. Consequently, those volumes remain **coverage unverified**, even where every EBSCO record is acquired. EBSCO does not establish historical coverage before September 2001 and can omit front/back matter.

The [JMU introduction record](https://commons.lib.jmu.edu/cad/vol38/iss1/3/) is available, but its PDF endpoint returned HTTP 403 during this run. Its unchanged pages were instead extracted from the already-local full issue.

## Rights evidence

Do not label the collection public domain. The website's existing quoted open-access policy remains intact, but the archived policy could not be independently fetched during this run. EBSCO copies carry a notice identifying CEDA as copyright holder and permitting individual downloading while restricting redistribution. Volume-38 article pages identify their authors as copyright holders and state Creative Commons Attribution Non-commercial No Derivatives licensing. These statements are source evidence, not a new rights determination. No deployment was performed.

## Additional findings — 2026-09-16

Two original volume-5 files were verified as mislabelled duplicates, with original bytes and URLs retained:

- Don Brownlee's *A projection of CEDA's near future* points to James E. Tomlinson's *The philosophy and development of CEDA*. A [reference in an ERIC-held paper](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED321309.pdf) identifies Brownlee's 1984 article on pages 93–98, but does not supply that article.
- Rich Simon's *The case against counterwarrants in value proposition debate* points to David M. Berube's *Debating hasty generalization*.

These two identity mismatches bring the known unresolved article count to four. See [exceptions](exceptions.md). Further searches found citations rather than replacement PDFs.

The [publisher-hosted anthology Perspectives in Controversy](https://idebate.net/Publications/PDFs/Perspectives%20in%20Controversy_%20Selected%20Essays%20from%20Contemporary%20Argumentation%20%26%20Debate%20-%20Kenneth%20Broda-Bahm.pdf) describes the 1980 and 1981 precursor collections and the transition to an annual CEDA Yearbook in 1982. This corroborates the earlier material's relationship to CAD, but is not a complete historical contents inventory.
